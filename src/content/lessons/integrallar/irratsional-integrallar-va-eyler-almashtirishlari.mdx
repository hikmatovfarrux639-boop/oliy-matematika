## Kirish: Ildizli ifodalarni ratsionallashtirish san'ati

Matematik analizda ildiz ostida o'zgaruvchi qatnashgan integrallar **irratsional integrallar** deyiladi. Integral hisobining oltin qoidasi shuki: *agar integral ostidagi ifodani qulay almashtirish yordamida ildizlardan butunlay xalos etib (ratsionallashtirib) olsak, uni odatiy ratsional kasrlarni integrallash qoidalari bilan osongina yechish mumkin*.

Ushbu darsda oliy o'quv yurtlari imtihonlarida eng ko'p tushadigan irratsional integrallarning asosiy turlarini va ularni yechishning universal usullarini, jumladan buyuk Leonard Eyler kashf etgan mashhur **Eyler almashtirishlari**ni batafsil tahlil qilamiz.

---

## 1. Oddiy chiziqli va kasr-chiziqli irratsional integrallar

### A. $\int R\left(x, x^{p_1/q_1}, x^{p_2/q_2}, \dots\right) dx$ ko'rinishidagi integrallar
Agar integral ostida bir xil $x$ argumentining turli kasr ko'rsatkichli ildizlari qatnashsa, barcha maxrajlarning eng kichik umumiy karralisi (EKUK) $k = \operatorname{EKUK}(q_1, q_2, \dots)$ topiladi va quyidagi almashtirish bajariladi:
$$
x = t^k \implies dx = k t^{k-1} dt
$$
Natijada barcha ildizlar butun darajalarga aylanadi.

### B. $\int R\left(x, \sqrt[n]{\frac{ax+b}{cx+d}}\right) dx$ ko'rinishidagi integrallar
Bir xil kasr-chiziqli ifodaning ildizi qatnashsa:
$$
\frac{ax+b}{cx+d} = t^n \implies ax+b = t^n(cx+d) \implies x = \frac{d t^n - b}{a - c t^n}
$$
Differensial $dx$ hisoblanadi va integral butunlay $t$ ga bog'liq ratsional kasrga aylanadi.

## 2. Kvadrat uchhadli irratsional integrallar: $\int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{ax^2+bx+c}}$

Bunday integrallarda ildiz ostidagi kvadrat uchhaddan **to'la kvadrat ajratiladi**:
$$
ax^2+bx+c = a\left[\left(x + \frac{b}{2a}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{c}{a} - \frac{b^2}{4a^2}\right)\right]
$$
Shundan so'ng $u = x + \frac{b}{2a}$ chiziqli almashtirish orqali integral quyidagi 3 ta jadval integralidan biriga keladi:
1. $\int \frac{du}{\sqrt{u^2 + k}} = \ln\left|u + \sqrt{u^2 + k}\right| + C$ ("uzun logarifm")
2. $\int \frac{du}{\sqrt{a^2 - u^2}} = \arcsin\left(\frac{u}{a}\right) + C$
3. Agar suratda chiziqli ifoda bo'lsa, $\int \frac{Ax+B}{\sqrt{ax^2+bx+c}} dx$, suratda ildiz ostining hosilasi $2ax+b$ sun'iy ajratib olinadi.

## 3. Eyler almashtirishlari (Klassik universal usul)

$\int R(x, \sqrt{ax^2+bx+c}) \, dx$ ko'rinishidagi ixtiyoriy integralni Eyler almashtirishlari yordamida ratsional funksiyaga keltirish mumkin. Leonard Eyler uchta holatni ajratgan:

```
                  Eyler almashtirishlari
                            |
       +--------------------+--------------------+
       |                    |                    |
 1-almashtirish       2-almashtirish       3-almashtirish
   (a > 0)              (c > 0)             (D > 0, ildizlar mavjud)
 \sqrt{ax^2+bx+c}     \sqrt{ax^2+bx+c}     \sqrt{a(x-x_1)(x-x_2)}
  = \pm\sqrt{a}x + t   = xt \pm \sqrt{c}     = t(x - x_1)
```

### 1-Eyler almashtirishi ($a > 0$ bo'lganda)
Agar bosh koeffitsiyent musbat bo'lsa:
$$
\sqrt{ax^2+bx+c} = \sqrt{a}\,x + t \quad \text{yoki} \quad -\sqrt{a}\,x + t
$$
Ikkala tomon kvadratga oshiriladi:
$$
ax^2+bx+c = ax^2 + 2\sqrt{a}xt + t^2 \implies bx - 2\sqrt{a}xt = t^2 - c \implies x = \frac{t^2 - c}{b - 2\sqrt{a}t}
$$
$x$ ning $t$ ga nisbatan chiziqli ifodasi topilgani sababli $dx$ ham ratsional bo'ladi va ildiz butunlay yo'qoladi!

### 2-Eyler almashtirishi ($c > 0$ bo'lganda)
Agar ozod had musbat bo'lsa:
$$
\sqrt{ax^2+bx+c} = xt + \sqrt{c}
$$
Kvadratga oshirganda:
$$
ax^2+bx+c = x^2 t^2 + 2xt\sqrt{c} + c \implies ax^2 - x^2 t^2 = 2xt\sqrt{c} - bx \implies x = \frac{2t\sqrt{c} - b}{a - t^2}
$$

### 3-Eyler almashtirishi ($ax^2+bx+c = a(x-x_1)(x-x_2)$ haqiqiy ildizlarga ega bo'lganda)
$$
\sqrt{a(x-x_1)(x-x_2)} = t(x - x_1)
$$
Kvadratga oshirib $x - x_1$ ga qisqartirilsa:
$$
a(x-x_2) = t^2(x-x_1) \implies x = \frac{a x_2 - t^2 x_1}{a - t^2}
$$

## 4. Trigonometrik almashtirishlar (Eylerga nisbatan tezkor alternativa)

Amaliyotda Eyler almashtirishlari ko'pincha juda katta darajali kasrlarni keltirib chiqaradi. Shu sababli ko'p hollarda **trigonometrik almashtirishlar** ancha sodda va ixcham hisoblanadi:

| Ildiz ko'rinishi | Tavsiya etiladigan almashtirish | Trigonometrik ayniyat |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}$ | $x = a \sin t \implies dx = a \cos t dt$ | $\sqrt{a^2 - a^2\sin^2 t} = a \cos t$ |
| $\sqrt{a^2 + x^2}$ | $x = a \operatorname{tg} t \implies dx = \frac{a \, dt}{\cos^2 t}$ | $\sqrt{a^2 + a^2\operatorname{tg}^2 t} = \frac{a}{\cos t}$ |
| $\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}$ | $x = \frac{a}{\cos t} \implies dx = \frac{a \sin t}{\cos^2 t} dt$ | $\sqrt{\frac{a^2}{\cos^2 t} - a^2} = a \operatorname{tg} t$ |

---

## 5. Masalalarni 2 xil usulda yechish namunalari

### 1-masala: Ildizli integralni Eyler va trigonometrik usulda yechish
Ushbu integralni hisoblang:
$$
I = \int \frac{dx}{x \sqrt{x^2 - 1}} \quad (x > 1)
$$

#### 1-usul: Trigonometrik almashtirish usuli
$x = \frac{1}{\cos t}$ deb olamiz, unda $dx = \frac{\sin t}{\cos^2 t} dt$.
Ildiz ifodasi:
$$
\sqrt{x^2 - 1} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{\cos^2 t} - 1} = \sqrt{\operatorname{tg}^2 t} = \operatorname{tg} t = \frac{\sin t}{\cos t}
$$
Integralga qo'yamiz:
$$
I = \int \frac{\frac{\sin t}{\cos^2 t} dt}{\frac{1}{\cos t} \cdot \frac{\sin t}{\cos t}} = \int \frac{\frac{\sin t}{\cos^2 t}}{\frac{\sin t}{\cos^2 t}} dt = \int 1 \, dt = t + C
$$
Eski o'zgaruvchiga qaytamiz: $x = \frac{1}{\cos t} \implies \cos t = \frac{1}{x} \implies t = \arccos\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)$.
Demak:
$$
I = \arccos\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) + C
$$

#### 2-usul: 1-Eyler almashtirishi usuli
Ildiz ostida $a = 1 > 0$. Demak 1-Eyler almashtirishini qo'llaymiz:
$$
\sqrt{x^2 - 1} = -x + t \implies x^2 - 1 = x^2 - 2xt + t^2 \implies 2xt = t^2 + 1 \implies x = \frac{t^2+1}{2t}
$$
Differensial:
$$
dx = \frac{2t \cdot 2t - (t^2+1) \cdot 2}{4t^2} dt = \frac{2(t^2-1)}{4t^2} dt = \frac{t^2-1}{2t^2} dt
$$
Ildiz ifodasi:
$$
\sqrt{x^2-1} = t - x = t - \frac{t^2+1}{2t} = \frac{t^2-1}{2t}
$$
Integralga qo'yamiz:
$$
I = \int \frac{\frac{t^2-1}{2t^2} dt}{\frac{t^2+1}{2t} \cdot \frac{t^2-1}{2t}} = \int \frac{\frac{t^2-1}{2t^2}}{\frac{(t^2+1)(t^2-1)}{4t^2}} dt = \int \frac{2}{t^2+1} dt = 2\operatorname{arctg}(t) + C
$$
Bu yerda $t = x + \sqrt{x^2-1}$. Natija: $2\operatorname{arctg}(x + \sqrt{x^2-1}) + C$.
*(Eslatma: $\arccos(1/x) = 2\operatorname{arctg}(x+\sqrt{x^2-1}) - \frac{\pi}{2}$, o'zgarmas $C$ hisobiga ikkala javob matematik jihatdan aynan tengdir).*

---

### 2-masala: Kasr-chiziqli irratsionallik
$$
\int \frac{\sqrt{x+1} + 2}{(x+1)^{2} - \sqrt{x+1}} \, dx
$$

**Yechish:**
Ildiz $\sqrt{x+1}$ barcha joyda bir xil. Shu sababli $x+1 = t^2$ almashtirishini kiritamiz ($t > 0$).
Unda $x = t^2 - 1 \implies dx = 2t \, dt$.
$$
\int \frac{t + 2}{t^4 - t} \cdot 2t \, dt = 2 \int \frac{t(t+2)}{t(t^3-1)} dt = 2 \int \frac{t+2}{t^3-1} dt
$$
Maxrajni ko'paytuvchilarga ajratamiz: $t^3 - 1 = (t-1)(t^2+t+1)$.
$$
\frac{t+2}{(t-1)(t^2+t+1)} = \frac{A}{t-1} + \frac{Bt+C}{t^2+t+1}
$$
$t=1$ qo'ysak: $3 = A(1+1+1) \implies 3 = 3A \implies A = 1$.
$t=0$ qo'ysak: $2 = A(1) + C(-1) \implies 2 = 1 - C \implies C = -1$.
$t^2$ koeffitsiyentlari: $0 = A + B \implies B = -A = -1$.
Demak:
$$
2 \int \left(\frac{1}{t-1} - \frac{t+1}{t^2+t+1}\right) dt
$$
Ikkinchi kasrning suratida maxraj hosilasi $(2t+1)$ ni ajratamiz:
$$
\frac{t+1}{t^2+t+1} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}(2t+1) + \frac{1}{2}}{t^2+t+1} = \frac{1}{2}\frac{2t+1}{t^2+t+1} + \frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{\left(t+\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{3}{4}}
$$
Integrallaymiz:
$$
2\ln|t-1| - \ln(t^2+t+1) - \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\operatorname{arctg}\left(\frac{2t+1}{\sqrt{3}}\right) + C
$$
Oxirida $t = \sqrt{x+1}$ qo'yiladi.

---

> [!WARNING] Imtihondagi tuzoqlar va ko'p uchraydigan xatoliklar
> 1. **Eyler almashtirishida $dx$ ni unutish:** Talabalar ko'pincha $x = \phi(t)$ ni topgach, to'g'ridan-to'g'ri $dx$ o'rniga shunchaki $dt$ yozib ketishadi. $dx = \phi'(t) dt$ bo'lishini hech qachon unutmang!
> 2. **Trigonometrik almashtirishda teskari qaytish:** $x = a\sin t$ deb yechib, oxirida javobda $t$ qolib ketadi. Javob doim boshlang'ich o'zgaruvchi $x$ orqali ifodalanishi shart.
> 3. **Ildiz ishorasi:** $\sqrt{u^2} = |u|$. Agar integrallash oralig'ida $u < 0$ bo'lsa, modulni minus ishora bilan ochish kerak.

## Mustaqil yechish uchun mashqlar

### 1-mashq
Ushbu integralni trigonometrik almashtirish yordamida hisoblang:
$$
\int \frac{\sqrt{4 - x^2}}{x^2} dx
$$

<details>
<summary>👉 Yechimni tekshirish</summary>

**Yechish:**
$x = 2\sin t$ deb olamiz, unda $dx = 2\cos t dt$, $\sqrt{4 - x^2} = 2\cos t$.
$$
\int \frac{2\cos t}{(2\sin t)^2} \cdot 2\cos t \, dt = \int \frac{4\cos^2 t}{4\sin^2 t} dt = \int \operatorname{ctg}^2 t \, dt = \int (\frac{1}{\sin^2 t} - 1) dt = -\operatorname{ctg} t - t + C
$$
Eski o'zgaruvchiga qaytamiz:
$\sin t = \frac{x}{2} \implies t = \arcsin\left(\frac{x}{2}\right)$.
$\operatorname{ctg} t = \frac{\cos t}{\sin t} = \frac{\sqrt{1 - \sin^2 t}}{\sin t} = \frac{\sqrt{1 - x^2/4}}{x/2} = \frac{\sqrt{4-x^2}}{x}$.

**Javob:**
$$
-\frac{\sqrt{4-x^2}}{x} - \arcsin\left(\frac{x}{2}\right) + C
$$
</details>

### 2-mashq
Ushbu integralni to'la kvadrat ajratish orqali hisoblang:
$$
\int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2 + 4x + 13}}
$$

<details>
<summary>👉 Yechimni tekshirish</summary>

**Yechish:**
Ildiz ostidan to'la kvadrat ajratamiz:
$$
x^2 + 4x + 13 = (x+2)^2 + 9
$$
$u = x+2 \implies du = dx$.
$$
\int \frac{du}{\sqrt{u^2 + 9}} = \ln\left|u + \sqrt{u^2 + 9}\right| + C
$$
O'rniga qo'ysak:
$$
\ln\left|x+2 + \sqrt{x^2+4x+13}\right| + C
$$
**Javob:** $\ln\left|x+2 + \sqrt{x^2+4x+13}\right| + C$.
</details>
