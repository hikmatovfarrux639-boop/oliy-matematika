## Kirish: Hosilaning teskari masalasi

Differensial hisobda bizga ma'lum bir $F(x)$ funksiya berilar edi va biz uning hosilasi $f(x) = F'(x)$ ni topishni o'rgandik. Masalan:
- Yo'l formulasi ma'lum bo'lsa, oniy tezlikni topdik: $v(t) = s'(t)$.
- Foyda funksiyasi ma'lum bo'lsa, marjinal foydani topdik.

Biroq real hayotda, fizikada va muhandislikda ko'pincha **teskari masala** qo'yiladi:
- Avtomobilning oniy tezligi $v(t)$ ma'lum, u bosib o'tgan umumiy masofani qanday tiklash mumkin?
- Idishga suv quyilish tezligi ma'lum bo'lsa, undagi suv hajmini qanday topamiz?
- Jismning sovush tezligidan uning haroratini qanday aniqlaymiz?

Hosilaga qarab dastlabki funksiyaning o'zini qayta tiklash masalasi **integral hisob** (lotincha *integrare* — butunlash, tiklash) deyiladi.

---

## 1. Boshlang'ich funksiya va nega $+ C$?

> **Ta'rif:** Agar berilgan oraliqdagi barcha $x$ lar uchun:
>
> $$
> F'(x) = f(x)
> $$
>
> tenglik bajarilsa, $F(x)$ funksiya $f(x)$ ning **boshlang'ich funksiyasi** deyiladi.

Masalan, $f(x) = 2x$ bo'lsa, uning boshlang'ich funksiyasi $F(x) = x^2$ bo'ladi, chunki $(x^2)' = 2x$.

Ammo quyidagilarga e'tibor bering:
- $(x^2 + 5)' = 2x + 0 = 2x$
- $(x^2 - 17)' = 2x - 0 = 2x$
- $(x^2 + 1000)' = 2x$
- $(x^2 + C)' = 2x$ (bu yerda $C$ — ixtiyoriy o'zgarmas son).

Har qanday o'zgarmas sonning hosilasi nolga teng bo'lgani sababli, berilgan bitta $f(x)$ funksiya uchun boshlang'ich funksiya bitta emas, balki **cheksiz ko'p** bo'ladi!

### Geometrik ma'nosi:
$y = F(x) + C$ — bu $Oy$ o'qi bo'ylab yuqoriga yoki pastga siljitilgan parallel egri chiziqlar oilasidir. Bu egri chiziqlarning bir xil $x$ nuqtadagi urinmalarining og'ish burchagi bir xil (ya'ni hosilalari teng) bo'ladi.

---

## 2. Aniqmas integral ta'rifi

> **Ta'rif:** $f(x)$ funksiyaning barcha boshlang'ich funksiyalari to'plamiga uning **aniqmas integrali** deyiladi va quyidagicha yoziladi:
>
> $$
> \int f(x) \, dx = F(x) + C
> $$
>
> bu yerda:
> - $\int$ — integral belgisi (lotincha *Summa* so'zining cho'zilgan $S$ harfi);
> - $f(x)$ — integral ostidagi funksiya;
> - $f(x) \, dx$ — integral ostidagi ifoda;
> - $dx$ — integrallash o'zgaruvchisi (differensial);
> - $C$ — ixtiyoriy integrallash o'zgarmasi (integrallash doimiysi).

---

## 3. Aniqmas integralning asosiy xossalari

1. **Hosila va integral bir-birini bekor qiladi:**
   $$
   \left( \int f(x) \, dx \right)' = f(x) \quad \text{va} \quad d\left(\int f(x) \, dx \right) = f(x) \, dx
   $$
2. **Differensialning integrali:**
   $$
   \int d(F(x)) = F(x) + C
   $$
3. **O'zgarmas ko'paytuvchini chiqarish:**
   $$
   \int k \cdot f(x) \, dx = k \int f(x) \, dx \quad (k \neq 0)
   $$
4. **Chiziqlilik (Yig'indi va ayirma qoidasi):**
   $$
   \int [f(x) \pm g(x)] \, dx = \int f(x) \, dx \pm \int g(x) \, dx
   $$

> ⚠️ **Qat'iy ogohlantirish:** Ko'paytma va bo'linma uchun bunday sodda qoida mavjud emas!
> $$\int f(x) g(x) \, dx \neq \int f(x) \, dx \cdot \int g(x) \, dx$$
> Ko'paytmalarni integrallash uchun maxsus o'zgaruvchini almashtirish yoki bo'laklab integrallash usullari qo'llaniladi.

---

## 4. Kengaytirilgan Asosiy Integrallar Jadvali

Universitet kursida talabalar bilishi shart bo'lgan to'liq jadval:

### Darajali va ko'rsatkichli funksiyalar:
| Funksiya | Aniqmas integral | Izoh |
|---|---|---|
| $0$ | $\int 0 \, dx = C$ | O'zgarmas son |
| $1$ (yoki $dx$) | $\int dx = x + C$ | |
| $x^n$ | $\int x^n \, dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C$ | **$n \neq -1$** sharti bilan |
| $\frac{1}{x}$ | $\int \frac{1}{x} \, dx = \ln\lvert x\rvert + C$ | Modul muhim! ($x \neq 0$) |
| $e^x$ | $\int e^x \, dx = e^x + C$ | O'zgarmas funksiya |
| $a^x$ | $\int a^x \, dx = \frac{a^x}{\ln a} + C$ | $a > 0, a \neq 1$ |

### Trigonometrik funksiyalar:
| Funksiya | Aniqmas integral | Izoh |
|---|---|---|
| $\cos x$ | $\int \cos x \, dx = \sin x + C$ | Musbat ishora |
| $\sin x$ | $\int \sin x \, dx = -\cos x + C$ | Manfiy ishora! |
| $\frac{1}{\cos^2 x}$ | $\int \frac{1}{\cos^2 x} \, dx = \operatorname{tg} x + C$ | |
| $\frac{1}{\sin^2 x}$ | $\int \frac{1}{\sin^2 x} \, dx = -\operatorname{ctg} x + C$ | Manfiy ishora! |
| $\operatorname{tg} x$ | $\int \operatorname{tg} x \, dx = -\ln\lvert\cos x\rvert + C$ | |
| $\operatorname{ctg} x$ | $\int \operatorname{ctg} x \, dx = \ln\lvert\sin x\rvert + C$ | |

### Maxsus kasr va ildizli integrallar (Imtihonda eng ko'p tushadiganlar):
| Funksiya | Aniqmas integral | Nomlanishi |
|---|---|---|
| $\frac{1}{x^2 + a^2}$ | $\int \frac{dx}{x^2 + a^2} = \frac{1}{a} \operatorname{arctg}\frac{x}{a} + C$ | Arktangens |
| $\frac{1}{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}}$ | $\int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}} = \arcsin\frac{x}{a} + C$ | Arksinus |
| $\frac{1}{x^2 - a^2}$ | $\int \frac{dx}{x^2 - a^2} = \frac{1}{2a} \ln\left\lvert\frac{x - a}{x + a}\right\rvert + C$ | **"Yuqori logarifm"** |
| $\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 \pm a^2}}$ | $\int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2 \pm a^2}} = \ln\left\lvert x + \sqrt{x^2 \pm a^2}\right\rvert + C$ | **"Uzun logarifm"** |

## 5. Integrallashdan oldin algebrali soddalashtirish usullari

Jadval formulasini to'g'ridan-to'g'ri qo'llashdan oldin ifodani algebraning oddiy usullari bilan yoyib olish kerak:

1. **Suratdagi har bir hadni maxrajga alohida bo'lish:**
   $$
   \int \frac{x^4 - 2x^2 + 5}{x^2} \, dx = \int \left( x^2 - 2 + \frac{5}{x^2} \right) \, dx
   $$
2. **Qavslarni ochish va ko'paytirish formulalarini qo'llash:**
   $$
   \int (2x - 3)^2 \, dx = \int (4x^2 - 12x + 9) \, dx
   $$
3. **Trigonometrik darajani pasaytirish formulalari:**
   $$
   \sin^2 x = \frac{1 - \cos 2x}{2}, \quad \cos^2 x = \frac{1 + \cos 2x}{2}
   $$

---

## 6. Amaliy misollar va batafsil yechimlar

### 1-misol: Ko'phadni integrallash
Hisoblang:
$$
\int (6x^3 - 4x^2 + 7x - 5) \, dx
$$

**Yechilishi:**
Xossalar bo'yicha har bir hadni alohida integrallaymiz:
$$
= 6 \cdot \frac{x^{3+1}}{4} - 4 \cdot \frac{x^{2+1}}{3} + 7 \cdot \frac{x^{1+1}}{2} - 5x + C
$$
$$
= \frac{3}{2}x^4 - \frac{4}{3}x^3 + \frac{7}{2}x^2 - 5x + C
$$

**Tekshirish:**
$$
\left(\frac{3}{2}x^4 - \frac{4}{3}x^3 + \frac{7}{2}x^2 - 5x + C\right)' = \frac{3}{2}(4x^3) - \frac{4}{3}(3x^2) + \frac{7}{2}(2x) - 5 = 6x^3 - 4x^2 + 7x - 5 \quad \checkmark
$$

---

### 2-misol: Kasr va manfiy darajalarni integrallash
Hisoblang:
$$
\int \left( \frac{3}{x^4} - \frac{2}{\sqrt[3]{x}} + \frac{5}{x} \right) \, dx
$$

**Yechilishi:**
Barcha ifodalarni $x^n$ daraja ko'rinishiga keltirib olamiz:
- $\frac{3}{x^4} = 3x^{-4}$
- $\frac{2}{\sqrt[3]{x}} = 2x^{-1/3}$
- $\frac{5}{x}$ — buni $5x^{-1}$ deb yozmaymiz, chunki darajali qoida $n = -1$ da ishlamaydi! Uning integrali $\ln|x|$ bo'ladi.

$$
\int (3x^{-4} - 2x^{-1/3} + \frac{5}{x}) \, dx = 3 \cdot \frac{x^{-4+1}}{-4+1} - 2 \cdot \frac{x^{-1/3+1}}{-\frac{1}{3} + 1} + 5\ln|x| + C
$$
$$
= 3 \cdot \frac{x^{-3}}{-3} - 2 \cdot \frac{x^{2/3}}{2/3} + 5\ln|x| + C = -\frac{1}{x^3} - 3\sqrt[3]{x^2} + 5\ln|x| + C
$$

**Javob:** $-\frac{1}{x^3} - 3\sqrt[3]{x^2} + 5\ln|x| + C$

---

### 3-misol: Hadma-had bo'lish usuli
Hisoblang:
$$
\int \frac{2x^3 - 5\sqrt{x} + 4}{x} \, dx
$$

**Yechilishi:**
Suratdagi har bir qo'shiluvchini maxrajdagi $x$ ga bo'lamiz:
$$
\frac{2x^3 - 5x^{1/2} + 4}{x} = 2x^2 - 5x^{-1/2} + \frac{4}{x}
$$
Endi integrallaymiz:
$$
\int \left( 2x^2 - 5x^{-1/2} + \frac{4}{x} \right) \, dx = 2 \cdot \frac{x^3}{3} - 5 \cdot \frac{x^{1/2}}{1/2} + 4\ln|x| + C
$$
$$
= \frac{2}{3}x^3 - 10\sqrt{x} + 4\ln|x| + C
$$

**Javob:** $\frac{2}{3}x^3 - 10\sqrt{x} + 4\ln|x| + C$

---

### 4-misol: Trigonometrik kvadratni integrallash
Hisoblang:
$$
\int \sin^2 x \, dx
$$

**Yechilishi:**
Jadvalda $\sin^2 x$ ning tayyor integrali yo'q. Shuning uchun yarim burchak (darajani pasaytirish) formulasidan foydalanamiz:
$$
\sin^2 x = \frac{1 - \cos 2x}{2}
$$
Integralga qo'yamiz:
$$
\int \frac{1 - \cos 2x}{2} \, dx = \frac{1}{2} \int 1 \, dx - \frac{1}{2} \int \cos 2x \, dx
$$
$\cos 2x$ ning integrali $\frac{1}{2}\sin 2x$ bo'ladi:
$$
= \frac{1}{2}x - \frac{1}{2} \left(\frac{1}{2}\sin 2x\right) + C = \frac{1}{2}x - \frac{1}{4}\sin 2x + C
$$

**Javob:** $\frac{1}{2}x - \frac{1}{4}\sin 2x + C$

---

### 5-misol: Arktangens va "Uzun logarifm"
Hisoblang:
$$
I_1 = \int \frac{dx}{x^2 + 9}, \quad I_2 = \int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2 + 5}}
$$

**Yechilishi:**
1. $I_1$ uchun jadvaldagi $\frac{1}{x^2 + a^2}$ formulasini qo'llaymiz ($a^2 = 9 \implies a = 3$):
   $$
   \int \frac{dx}{x^2 + 3^2} = \frac{1}{3} \operatorname{arctg}\frac{x}{3} + C
   $$
2. $I_2$ uchun jadvaldagi "uzun logarifm" formulasini qo'llaymiz ($a^2 = 5$):
   $$
   \int \frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2 + 5}} = \ln\left\lvert x + \sqrt{x^2 + 5}\right\rvert + C
   $$

---

## ⚠️ Talabalar ko'p yo'l qo'yadigan 3 ta jiddiy xato

1. **Integrallash doimiysi $+ C$ ni unutish:**
   > Aniqmas integral bitta funksiya emas, balki funksiyalar to'plamidir. Har qanday aniqmas integral oxirida $+ C$ qo'yilishi shart, aks holda imtihonda o'qituvchi ballni kesadi.

2. **$\int \frac{1}{x} \, dx$ da modulni tushirib qoldirish:**
   > $\int \frac{1}{x} \, dx = \ln(x) + C$ deb yozish to'liq to'g'ri emas, chunki $x$ manfiy bo'lishi ham mumkin. To'g'ri yozilishi: $\ln|x| + C$.

3. **Ko'paytma va bo'linmani hadma-had integrallash:**
   > $\int (x \cdot \sin x) \, dx = \frac{x^2}{2} \cdot (-\cos x)$ deb yozish mutlaqo xato! Ko'paytmali integrallarni yechish uchun bo'laklab integrallash usuli kerak.

---

## 💡 Ustozdan maslahat: Javobingizni 100% tekshirish usuli

Integralni to'g'ri yechganingizga ishonch hosil qilish uchun topilgan natijadan **hosila oling**:
$$
(F(x) + C)' = f(x)
$$
Agar olingan hosila integral ostidagi funksiyaga aynan teng chiqsa, demak siz misolni 100% benuqson bajargansiz!
