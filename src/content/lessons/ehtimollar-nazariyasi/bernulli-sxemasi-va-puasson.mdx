## Kirish: Qaytariluvchi tajribalar

Agar tanga 1 marta emas, balki 100 marta tashlansa, aynan 50 marta raqam tomoni tushishi ehtimoli qancha? Yoki zavodda 10 000 ta mikrosxema ishlab chiqarilganda, ularning aynan 3 tasi yaroqsiz chiqishi ehtimolini qanday hisoblash mumkin?

Har bir sinovda hodisaning ro'y berish ehtimoli o'zgarmasdan saqlanadigan bunday mustaqil takroriy sinovlar zanjiri **Bernulli sxemasi** deb ataladi.

---

## 1. Bernulli formulasi

Faraz qilaylik, $n$ ta mustaqil sinov o'tkazilmoqda. Har bir sinovda:
- $A$ hodisaning ro'y berish (muvaffaqiyat) ehtimoli: $P(A) = p$;
- $A$ hodisaning ro'y bermaslik (muvaffaqiyatsizlik) ehtimoli: $q = 1 - p$.

> **Bernulli formulasi:**
> $n$ ta mustaqil sinovda $A$ hodisaning **aynan $k$ marta** ro'y berishi ehtimoli:
>
> $$
> P_n(k) = C_n^k \cdot p^k \cdot q^{n - k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n - k)!} p^k q^{n - k}
> $$

---

## 2. Eng katta ehtimolli son ($k_0$)

$n$ ta sinovda hodisaning ro'y berish ehtimoli qaysi $k$ sonda eng maksimal qiymatga erishadi? Bu son **eng katta ehtimolli son** $k_0$ deyiladi va quyidagi qo'shaloq tengsizlikdan topiladi:

$$
n p - q \le k_0 \le n p + p
$$

Oradagi masofa $(np + p) - (np - q) = p + q = 1$ ga teng bo'lganligi sababli, bu oraliqda odatda yagona bitta butun son yotadi.

---

## 3. Puasson asimptotik formulasi (Kam uchraydigan hodisalar)

Agar sinovlar soni $n$ juda katta ($n \to \infty$) va hodisaning har bir sinovda ro'y berish ehtimoli $p$ juda kichik ($p \to 0$) bo'lsa, Bernulli formulasidagi faktoriallarni hisoblash kompyuterlar uchun ham qiyinlashadi.

Bunday holatda $\lambda = n \cdot p$ ko'paytma o'zgarmas deb olinadi va **Puasson formulasi** qo'llaniladi:

$$
P_n(k) \approx \frac{\lambda^k \cdot e^{-\lambda}}{k!} \quad (\lambda = n \cdot p)
$$

### Qo'llanish sohalari:
- Serverga sekundiga kelib tushadigan so'rovlar soni (DDoS yoki odatiy trafik);
- Yo'l-transport hodisalari soni;
- Matbaa kitobidagi bosma xatolar soni;
- Bankomatdagi navbatlar.

---

## 4. Muavr-Laplas teoremalari ($n$ katta, $p$ o'rtacha bo'lganda)

Agar $n$ katta bo'lib, $p$ nolga yoki birga juda yaqin bo'lmasa ($npq \ge 10$):

### 1. Laplasning lokal formulasi (aynan $k$ marta ro'y berishi):
$$
P_n(k) \approx \frac{1}{\sqrt{npq}} \cdot \varphi(x), \quad x = \frac{k - np}{\sqrt{npq}}
$$
bu yerda $\varphi(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{x^2}{2}}$ — standart Gauss funksiyasi (maxsus jadvallardan olinadi).

### 2. Laplasning integral formulasi ($k_1$ dan $k_2$ gacha oraliqda):
$$
P(k_1 \le k \le k_2) \approx \Phi\left(\frac{k_2 - np}{\sqrt{npq}}\right) - \Phi\left(\frac{k_1 - np}{\sqrt{npq}}\right)
$$
bu yerda $\Phi(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int_0^x e^{-\frac{t^2}{2}} dt$ — Laplas funksiyasi.

## Amaliy namunaviy misollar

### 1-misol: Bernulli formulasi
Tanga 5 marta tashlandi. Aynan 3 marta gerb tushishi ehtimolini hisoblang.

**Yechilishi:**
$n = 5, \, k = 3, \, p = 0.5, \, q = 0.5$.
$$P_5(3) = C_5^3 \cdot (0.5)^3 \cdot (0.5)^{5-3} = \frac{5 \cdot 4}{2 \cdot 1} \cdot (0.5)^5 = 10 \cdot \frac{1}{32} = \frac{10}{32} = \frac{5}{16} = 0.3125 \quad (31.25\%)$$

---

### 2-misol: Puasson formulasi
Zavodda har 1000 ta mahsulotdan o'rtacha 2 tasi yaroqsiz chiqadi ($p = 0.002$). 500 ta mahsulot orasida aynan 1 ta yaroqsiz mahsulot bo'lishi ehtimolini toping.

**Yechilishi:**
1. Parametrni topamiz: $\lambda = n \cdot p = 500 \cdot 0.002 = 1$.
2. Puasson formulasiga $k = 1$ ni qo'yamiz:
   $$P_{500}(1) \approx \frac{1^1 \cdot e^{-1}}{1!} = \frac{1}{e} \approx \frac{1}{2.718} \approx 0.368 \quad (36.8\%)$$

---
