## Bernulli tenglamasi nima?

Universitet differensial tenglamalar kursida chiziqli bo'lmagan, lekin maxsus usul bilan chiziqli tenglamaga osongina keltiriladigan eng mashhur tenglama bu **Bernulli tenglamasi**dir.

Ushbu ko'rinishdagi 1-tartibli differensial tenglama Bernulli tenglamasi deyiladi:

$$
y' + P(x)y = Q(x) \cdot y^n
$$

Bu yerda:
- $P(x)$ va $Q(x)$ — $x$ ga bog'liq uzluksiz funksiyalar (yoki o'zgarmas sonlar).
- $n$ — ixtiyoriy haqiqiy son.

> **Muhim eslatma:**
> - Agar $n = 0$ bo'lsa: $y' + P(x)y = Q(x)$ oddiy **chiziqli tenglama** bo'ladi.
> - Agar $n = 1$ bo'lsa: $y' + [P(x) - Q(x)]y = 0$ **o'zgaruvchilari ajraladigan** tenglama bo'ladi.
> - Shu sababli, Bernulli tenglamasi faqat **$n \neq 0$ va $n \neq 1$** bo'lganda o'rganiladi!

---

## 1-Usul: Yangi o'zgaruvchi kiritish ($z = y^{1-n}$)

Bu klassik usul bo'lib, uning yordamida tenglama to'g'ridan-to'g'ri chiziqli tenglamaga aylanadi:

1. Tenglamaning ikkala tomonini $y^n$ ga bo'lamiz:
   $$y^{-n} y' + P(x) y^{1-n} = Q(x)$$

2. Yangi o'zgaruvchi kiritamiz:
   $$z = y^{1-n}$$
   Uning hosilasi:
   $$z' = (1 - n) y^{-n} y' \implies y^{-n} y' = \frac{z'}{1 - n}$$

3. Tenglamaga qo'yamiz:
   $$\frac{z'}{1 - n} + P(x) z = Q(x) \implies z' + (1 - n)P(x) z = (1 - n)Q(x)$$

Bu esa $z(x)$ funksiyaga nisbatan **odatiy 1-tartibli chiziqli differensial tenglamadir**! Uni yechib $z$ topiladi, so'ngra $y = z^{\frac{1}{1-n}}$ orqali $y$ ga qaytiladi.

---

## 2-Usul: Bernulli usuli ($y = u \cdot v$)

Ko'pchilik talabalar va ustozlar $y = u(x) \cdot v(x)$ ko'paytma almashtirish usulini afzal ko'rishadi, chunki bu usul yangi $z$ formulasini yodda saqlashni talab qilmaydi:

1. $y = uv \implies y' = u'v + uv'$ deb olinadi.
2. Tenglamaga qo'yiladi:
   $$u'v + uv' + P(x)uv = Q(x) (uv)^n \implies u'v + u(v' + P(x)v) = Q(x) u^n v^n$$
3. $v' + P(x)v = 0$ shartdan $v(x)$ topiladi.
4. Qolgan tenglama $u$ ga nisbatan o'zgaruvchilari ajraladigan tenglamaga aylanadi.

---

## Amaliy namunaviy misol

Ushbu Bernulli tenglamasini to'liq yeching:

$$
y' + \frac{y}{x} = x y^2
$$

### Yechilishi ($y = uv$ usuli):

Bu yerda $P(x) = \frac{1}{x}$, $Q(x) = x$, $n = 2$.
Shuningdek, $y = 0$ yechim ekanligini darhol payqash mumkin.

**1-qadam:** $y = uv$ va $y' = u'v + uv'$ almashtiramiz:
$$u'v + uv' + \frac{uv}{x} = x u^2 v^2$$
$$u'v + u\left(v' + \frac{v}{x}\right) = x u^2 v^2$$

**2-qadam:** Qavs ichini nolga tenglashtirib, $v(x)$ ni topamiz:
$$v' + \frac{v}{x} = 0 \implies \frac{dv}{dx} = -\frac{v}{x} \implies \frac{dv}{v} = -\frac{dx}{x}$$
Integrallaymiz:
$$\ln|v| = -\ln|x| = \ln\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) \implies v = \frac{1}{x}$$

**3-qadam:** Topilgan $v = \frac{1}{x}$ ni asosiy tenglamaga qo'yamiz:
$$u' \cdot \frac{1}{x} = x u^2 \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^2 \implies u' \cdot \frac{1}{x} = \frac{x u^2}{x^2} = \frac{u^2}{x}$$
Ikkala tomonni $x$ ga ko'paytiramiz:
$$u' = u^2 \implies \frac{du}{dx} = u^2$$

**4-qadam:** O'zgaruvchilarni ajratamiz:
$$\frac{du}{u^2} = dx \implies \int u^{-2} du = \int dx$$
$$-\frac{1}{u} = x + C \implies u = -\frac{1}{x + C}$$

**5-qadam:** Umumiy yechimni tuzamiz ($y = uv$):
$$y = \left(-\frac{1}{x + C}\right) \cdot \frac{1}{x} = -\frac{1}{x(x + C)}$$

**Javob:** $y = -\frac{1}{x^2 + Cx}$ va $y = 0$.

## Mustaqil yechish uchun mashqlar

1. $y' + 2xy = 2x y^3$ Bernulli tenglamasida $n$ nimaga teng va qanday almashtirish kiritiladi?
2. $x y' + y = y^2 \ln x$ tenglamasini Bernulli usuli ($y = uv$) bilan yeching.
