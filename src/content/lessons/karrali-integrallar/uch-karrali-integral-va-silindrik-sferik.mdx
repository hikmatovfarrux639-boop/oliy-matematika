## Kirish: Haqiqiy uch o'lchamli dunyo

Agar jism bir jinsli bo'lmasa — ya'ni uning zichligi har bir nuqtada turlicha o'zgarsa (masalan, Yer sayyorasining zichligi markazga yaqinlashgan sari ortadi), uning umumiy massasini, og'irlik markazini yoki inersiya momentini oddiy ko'paytirish bilan topib bo'lmaydi.

Bunday fazoviy hisoblashlar uchun **uch karrali integral** va uch o'lchamli egri chiziqli koordinatalar (silindrik va sferik) qo'llaniladi.

---

## 1. Uch karrali integral ta'rifi

Fazoviy uch o'lchamli $V$ soha va unda aniqlangan uzluksiz $f(x, y, z)$ funksiya berilgan bo'lsin.
$V$ sohani mayda $\Delta V_i$ bo'lakchalarga bo'lib, integral yig'indining limitini olsak, **uch karrali integral** hosil bo'ladi:

$$
\iiint_V f(x, y, z) \, dx dy dz = \lim_{\operatorname{diam} \to 0} \sum_{i=1}^n f(\xi_i, \eta_i, \zeta_i) \Delta V_i
$$

### Tatbiqlari:
1. **Fazoviy jism hajmi:**
   $$
   V = \iiint_V dx dy dz
   $$
2. **Jism massasi:** Agar $\rho(x, y, z)$ zichlik funksiyasi bo'lsa:
   $$
   M = \iiint_V \rho(x, y, z) \, dx dy dz
   $$

---

## 2. Silindrik koordinatalar sistemasi

Agar integrallash sohasi **silindr, konus yoki paraboloid** shaklida bo'lsa, silindrik koordinatalar eng qulayi hisoblanadi. U tekislikdagi qutb koordinatalari va vertikal $z$ o'qining birikmasidir:

$$
\begin{cases}
x = r \cos \varphi \\
y = r \sin \varphi \\
z = z
\end{cases}
$$
bu yerda $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$.

### Hajm elementi:
$$
dx dy dz = r \, dr d\varphi dz
$$

---

## 3. Sferik koordinatalar sistemasi

Agar integrallash sohasi **shar yoki shar qatlami** ($x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \le R^2$) bo'lsa, sferik koordinatalar ishlatiladi:
- $\rho$ — fazoviy radius (koordinata boshidan nuqtagacha masofa, $\rho \ge 0$);
- $\theta$ — zenit burchagi ($Oz$ o'qi bilan hosil qilgan burchak, $0 \le \theta \le \pi$);
- $\varphi$ — azimut burchagi ($Ox$ o'qi bilan tekislikdagi burchak, $0 \le \varphi < 2\pi$).

### O'tish formulalari:
$$
\begin{cases}
x = \rho \sin \theta \cos \varphi \\
y = \rho \sin \theta \sin \varphi \\
z = \rho \cos \theta
\end{cases}
$$
bu yerda $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = \rho^2$.

### Hajm elementi (Sferik Yakobian):
$$
dx dy dz = \rho^2 \sin \theta \, d\rho d\theta d\varphi
$$

---

## 4. Klassik isbot: Shar hajmi qayerdan kelgan?

Maktab geometriya darslaridan ma'lumki, radiusi $R$ bo'lgan shar hajmi $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi R^3$ formulasi bilan hisoblanadi. Endi bu formulani sferik koordinatalar yordamida qat'iy isbotlaymiz!

Shar uchun chegaralar:
- $0 \le \rho \le R$;
- $0 \le \theta \le \pi$;
- $0 \le \varphi \le 2\pi$.

$$
V = \iiint_V dx dy dz = \int_0^{2\pi} d\varphi \int_0^\pi \sin \theta \, d\theta \int_0^R \rho^2 \, d\rho
$$
Uchala mustaqil integralni hisoblaymiz:
1. $\int_0^{2\pi} d\varphi = 2\pi$
2. $\int_0^\pi \sin \theta \, d\theta = [-\cos \theta]_0^\pi = -(-1) - (-1) = 2$
3. $\int_0^R \rho^2 \, d\rho = \left[ \frac{\rho^3}{3} \right]_0^R = \frac{R^3}{3}$

Barchasini ko'paytiramiz:
$$
V = 2\pi \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{R^3}{3} = \frac{4}{3} \pi R^3
$$
Formula to'liq isbotlandi!

## Amaliy namunaviy misol

Hisoblang:
$$
\iiint_V z \, dx dy dz
$$
bu yerda $V$ — $x = 0, y = 0, z = 0, x + y + z = 1$ tekisliklar bilan chegaralangan tetraedr (piramida).

**Yechilishi:**
Chegaralarni aniqlaymiz:
- $0 \le x \le 1$
- $0 \le y \le 1 - x$
- $0 \le z \le 1 - x - y$

$$
I = \int_0^1 dx \int_0^{1-x} dy \int_0^{1-x-y} z \, dz
$$
1. $z$ bo'yicha ichki integral:
   $$\int_0^{1-x-y} z \, dz = \frac{(1 - x - y)^2}{2}$$
2. $y$ bo'yicha o'rta integral:
   $$\frac{1}{2} \int_0^{1-x} (1 - x - y)^2 dy = \frac{1}{2} \left[ -\frac{(1 - x - y)^3}{3} \right]_0^{1-x} = \frac{1}{6}(1 - x)^3$$
3. $x$ bo'yicha tashqi integral:
   $$\frac{1}{6} \int_0^1 (1 - x)^3 dx = \frac{1}{6} \left[ -\frac{(1 - x)^4}{4} \right]_0^1 = \frac{1}{24}$$

**Javob:** $\frac{1}{24}$.
