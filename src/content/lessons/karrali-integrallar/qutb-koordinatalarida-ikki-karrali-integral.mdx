## Kirish: Doirada Dekart koordinatalari noqulay

Agar integrallash sohasi to'g'ri to'rtburchak emas, balki **aylana, doira yoki doiraviy halqa** ($x^2 + y^2 \le R^2$) bo'lsa, Dekart koordinatalarida ishlash juda og'ir kechadi: chegaralarda noqulay $\pm\sqrt{R^2 - x^2}$ ildizlar paydo bo'ladi.

Bunday aylanma simmetriyaga ega sohalarda integrallashni osonlashtirish uchun **qutb koordinatalari sistemasiga** o'tiladi.

---

## 1. Qutb koordinatalari formulalari

Tekislikdagi har bir nuqta ikkita kattalik bilan aniqlanadi:
- $r$ — qutb radiusi (koordinata boshidan nuqtagacha bo'lgan masofa, $r \ge 0$);
- $\varphi$ — qutb burchagi ($Ox$ o'qining musbat yo'nalishi bilan hosil qilgan burchak, $0 \le \varphi < 2\pi$).

### O'tish formulalari:
$$
\begin{cases}
x = r \cos \varphi \\
y = r \sin \varphi
\end{cases}
$$
Bu holda:
$$
x^2 + y^2 = (r\cos\varphi)^2 + (r\sin\varphi)^2 = r^2(\cos^2\varphi + \sin^2\varphi) = r^2
$$

---

## 2. Yuza elementi va Yakobian siri

O'zgaruvchilarni almashtirganda yuza elementi $dx dy$ shunchaki $dr d\varphi$ ga teng bo'lib qolmaydi! Ular orasidagi mutanosiblik koeffitsiyenti — **almashtirish Yakobiani ($J$)** deb ataladi:

$$
J = \begin{vmatrix}
\frac{\partial x}{\partial r} & \frac{\partial x}{\partial \varphi} \\
\frac{\partial y}{\partial r} & \frac{\partial y}{\partial \varphi}
\end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix}
\cos \varphi & -r \sin \varphi \\
\sin \varphi & r \cos \varphi
\end{vmatrix} = r \cos^2 \varphi - (-r \sin^2 \varphi) = r(\cos^2 \varphi + \sin^2 \varphi) = r
$$

> **Oltin qoida (Talabalarning eng katta xatosi):**
> Qutb koordinatalariga o'tganda $dx dy$ o'rniga doimo **$r \, dr d\varphi$** yozilishi shart:
>
> $$
> dx dy = r \, dr d\varphi
> $$
>
> Integral formulasi:
> $$
> \iint_D f(x, y) \, dx dy = \iint_{D^*} f(r\cos\varphi, \, r\sin\varphi) \cdot r \, dr d\varphi
> $$

---

## 3. Doira bo'yicha integrallash chegaralari

Agar $D$ markazi koordinatalar boshida va radiusi $R$ bo'lgan to'liq doira bo'lsa ($x^2 + y^2 \le R^2$):
- $\varphi$ burchak to'liq bir aylanadi: $0 \le \varphi \le 2\pi$;
- $r$ radius markazdan aylanagacha o'zgaradi: $0 \le r \le R$.

Takroriy integralga keltirish:
$$
\iint_D f(x^2 + y^2) \, dx dy = \int_0^{2\pi} d\varphi \int_0^R f(r^2) \cdot r \, dr
$$

---

## 4. Matematika durdonasi: Puasson-Gauss integrali

Ehtimollar nazariyasida va kvant mexanikasida eng muhim integral:
$$
I = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-x^2} \, dx
$$
Uning boshlang'ich funksiyasini elementar funksiyalarda ifodalab bo'lmaydi (Liuvill teoremasi). Ammo ikki karrali integral va qutb koordinatalari yordamida uning aniq qiymatini hisoblash mumkin!

### Isbot:
Kvadratini olamiz:
$$
I^2 = \left(\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-x^2} dx\right) \left(\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-y^2} dy\right) = \iint_{\mathbb{R}^2} e^{-(x^2 + y^2)} \, dx dy
$$
Qutb koordinatalariga o'tamiz ($x^2 + y^2 = r^2, \, dx dy = r dr d\varphi$):
$$
I^2 = \int_0^{2\pi} d\varphi \int_0^\infty e^{-r^2} \cdot r \, dr
$$
- Burchak bo'yicha: $\int_0^{2\pi} d\varphi = 2\pi$.
- Radius bo'yicha ($r dr = \frac{1}{2} d(r^2)$):
  $$\int_0^\infty e^{-r^2} r dr = \frac{1}{2} \left[ -e^{-r^2} \right]_0^\infty = \frac{1}{2} (0 - (-1)) = \frac{1}{2}$$
Ko'paytiramiz:
$$
I^2 = 2\pi \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \pi \implies I = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

**Natija:** $\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} e^{-x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi}$. Bu matematikaning eng go'zal natijalaridan biridir!

---

## Amaliy namunaviy misol

Hisoblang:
$$
\iint_D \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} \, dx dy
$$
bu yerda $D$ — markazi $(0, 0)$ va radiusi $R = 3$ bo'lgan doira.

**Yechilishi:**
1. Qutb koordinatalariga o'tamiz:
   $\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = \sqrt{r^2} = r$.
   $dx dy = r \, dr d\varphi$.
2. Chegaralar: $0 \le \varphi \le 2\pi$, $0 \le r \le 3$.
3. Integralni tuzamiz:
   $$I = \int_0^{2\pi} d\varphi \int_0^3 r \cdot (r \, dr) = \left( \int_0^{2\pi} d\varphi \right) \left( \int_0^3 r^2 \, dr \right)$$
4. Hisoblaymiz:
   $$\int_0^{2\pi} d\varphi = 2\pi$$
   $$\int_0^3 r^2 \, dr = \left[ \frac{r^3}{3} \right]_0^3 = \frac{27}{3} = 9$$
5. Ko'paytma:
   $$I = 2\pi \cdot 9 = 18\pi$$

**Javob:** $18\pi$.
