## Kirish: Kichik o'zgarishlarning yig'indisi

Agar siz do'kondan to'g'ri to'rtburchak shaklidagi gilam sotib olayotgan bo'lsangiz, uning yuzi $S = x \cdot y$ bo'ladi. Agar gilamning eni ham ($\Delta x$), bo'yi ham ($\Delta y$) bir necha santimetrga o'zgarsa, umumiy yuza qanchaga o'zgaradi? 

Faqat bitta argument bo'yicha hosila bu o'zgarishni to'liq ifodalay olmaydi. Ikkala argument ham bir vaqtda o'zgarganda funksiya qiymatining bosh chiziqli o'zgarish qismini topish uchun **to'liq differensial** tushunchasi qo'llaniladi.

---

## 1. To'liq orttirma va differensiallanuvchanlik

Argumentlar mos ravishda $\Delta x$ va $\Delta y$ orttirma olganda, funksiyaning **to'liq orttirmasi**:
$$
\Delta z = f(x + \Delta x, y + \Delta y) - f(x, y)
$$

Agar to'liq orttirmani quyidagi ko'rinishda yozish mumkin bo'lsa:
$$
\Delta z = A \cdot \Delta x + B \cdot \Delta y + \alpha \cdot \Delta x + \beta \cdot \Delta y
$$
(bu yerda $\Delta x, \Delta y \to 0$ da $\alpha, \beta \to 0$ cheksiz kichik miqdorlar), u holda $f(x, y)$ funksiya **differensiallanuvchi** deyiladi.

Ushbu ifodaning $\Delta x$ va $\Delta y$ larga nisbatan bosh chiziqli qismi $A \Delta x + B \Delta y$ funksiyaning **to'liq differensiali** deb ataladi va $A = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x}$, $B = \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$ bo'ladi.

---

## 2. To'liq differensial formulasi

Erkli o'zgaruvchilar uchun $dx = \Delta x$ va $dy = \Delta y$ deb qabul qilinganligi sababli, ikki o'zgaruvchili funksiyaning **to'liq differensiali**:

$$
dz = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} dx + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} dy
$$

Agar funksiya uchta o'zgaruvchiga bog'liq bo'lsa ($u = f(x, y, z)$):
$$
du = \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} dx + \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} dy + \frac{\partial u}{\partial z} dz
$$

---

## 3. Taqribiy hisoblash formulasi

$\Delta x$ va $\Delta y$ yetarlicha kichik bo'lganda, funksiya to'liq orttirmasi uning to'liq differensialiga deyarli teng bo'ladi:
$$
\Delta z \approx dz
$$

Bundan taqribiy hisoblashning asosiy amaliy formulasi kelib chiqadi:

$$
f(x_0 + \Delta x, \, y_0 + \Delta y) \approx f(x_0, y_0) + f'_x(x_0, y_0) \Delta x + f'_y(x_0, y_0) \Delta y
$$

Ushbu formula murakkab funksiyalarning (ildizlar, darajalar, logarifmlar) qiymatini kalkulyatorsiz yuqori aniqlikda tez hisoblash imkonini beradi!

---

## 4. O'lchovlardagi xatoliklarni baholash

Fizik va muhandislik tajribalarida $x$ va $y$ kattaliklar $\Delta x$ va $\Delta y$ xatoliklar bilan o'lchangan bo'lsa:
- **Absolyut xatolik:**
  $$
  \Delta z \approx \left\lvert \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \right\rvert \Delta x + \left\lvert \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \right\rvert \Delta y
  $$
- **Nisbiy xatolik:**
  $$
  \delta z = \frac{\Delta z}{\lvert z \rvert} \approx \left\lvert \frac{\partial \ln z}{\partial x} \right\rvert \Delta x + \left\lvert \frac{\partial \ln z}{\partial y} \right\rvert \Delta y
  $$

## Amaliy namunaviy misollar

### 1-misol: To'liq differensialni topish
$z = x^2 y^3 - 3xy + 4$ funksiyaning to'liq differensialini toping.

**Yechilishi:**
1. Xususiy hosilalarni hisoblaymiz:
   $$z'_x = 2xy^3 - 3y$$
   $$z'_y = 3x^2 y^2 - 3x$$
2. To'liq differensial formulasiga qo'yamiz:
   $$dz = (2xy^3 - 3y) dx + (3x^2 y^2 - 3x) dy$$

---

### 2-misol: Taqribiy hisoblash
Taqribiy hisoblang:
$$
(1.02)^3 \cdot (1.97)^2
$$

**Yechilishi:**
1. Funksiya tanlaymiz: $f(x, y) = x^3 y^2$.
2. Qulay boshlang'ich nuqtalarni tanlaymiz: $x_0 = 1, \, y_0 = 2$.
   Unda: $f(x_0, y_0) = 1^3 \cdot 2^2 = 4$.
3. Orttirmalarni topamiz:
   $$\Delta x = 1.02 - 1 = +0.02$$
   $$\Delta y = 1.97 - 2 = -0.03$$
4. Xususiy hosilalar va ularning $(1, 2)$ dagi qiymatlari:
   $$f'_x = 3x^2 y^2 \implies f'_x(1, 2) = 3(1)^2(2)^2 = 12$$
   $$f'_y = 2x^3 y \implies f'_y(1, 2) = 2(1)^3(2) = 4$$
5. Taqribiy formulaga qo'yamiz:
   $$f(1.02, 1.97) \approx 4 + 12 \cdot (0.02) + 4 \cdot (-0.03)$$
   $$= 4 + 0.24 - 0.12 = 4.12$$

(Kalkulyatorda haqiqiy qiymat: $1.061208 \cdot 3.8809 \approx 4.118$, xatolik atigi $0.002$ yoki $0.05\%$).

## Mustaqil yechish uchun mashqlar

1. $z = \sin(x^2 + y^2)$ funksiyaning to'liq differensiali $dz$ ni toping.
2. $z = \frac{x}{y}$ bo'lsa, $(2, 1)$ nuqtada $dz$ ni toping.
3. $\sqrt{(3.04)^2 + (3.98)^2}$ qiymatni to'liq differensial yordamida taqribiy hisoblang (Maslahat: $x_0=3, y_0=4$).
