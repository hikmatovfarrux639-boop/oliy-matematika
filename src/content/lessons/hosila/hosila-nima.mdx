## 📌 Kirish va Geometrik/Fizik Mohiyat

Koinotdagi barcha hodisalar uzluksiz harakat va o'zgarishdadir: sayyoralarning quyosh atrofida aylanishi, reaktiv samolyotning parvozi, iqtisodiyotdagi inflyatsiya sur'ati yoki elektr zanjiridagi tok kuchi. Maktab matematikasida biz o'rtacha tezlikni hisoblashga o'rganganmiz:

$$
v_{\text{o'rt}} = \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta t}
$$

Agar avtomobil $120\text{ km}$ masofani $2\text{ soatda}$ bosib o'tsa, uning o'rtacha tezligi $60\text{ km/soat}$ bo'ladi. Ammo bu ko'rsatkich avtomobil har soniyada aynan $60\text{ km/soat}$ bilan yurganini bildirmaydi — u qizil chiroqda to'xtagan, trassada esa $110\text{ km/soat}$ tezlikda harakatlangan bo'lishi mumkin. 

Tabiiy savol tug'iladi: **harakatning ayni bir lahzadagi (oniy paytdagi) haqiqiy tezligi qanday aniqlanadi?**

Buning uchun vaqt oralig'i $\Delta t$ ni cheksiz kichraytirib, uni nolga intiltirishimiz kerak ($\Delta t \to 0$):

$$
v(t) = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta t} = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{s(t + \Delta t) - s(t)}{\Delta t}
$$

Mana shu jarayon — ya'ni argument cheksiz kichik o'zgarganda funksiyaning o'zgarish sur'atini o'lchash — insoniyat tarixidagi eng buyuk ilmiy kashfiyotlardan biri bo'lgan **differensial hisob** va **hosila** tushunchasining asosidir. Ushbu nazariya XVII asr oxirida bir-biridan mustaqil ravishda Isaak Nyuton (mexanika va harakat orqali) va Gotfrid Vilgelm Leybnits (geometriya va urinmalar masalasi orqali) tomonidan kashf etilgan.

### Geometrik ma'no: Kesuvchidan urinmaga qarab

Faraz qilaylik, silliq $y = f(x)$ egri chiziq berilgan. Uning ustida ikkita nuqtani olaylik: qo'zg'almas $M_0(x_0, f(x_0))$ va qo'zg'aluvchi $M(x_0 + \Delta x, f(x_0 + \Delta x))$. 

Ushbu ikki nuqta orqali $M_0 M$ kesuvchi to'g'ri chiziq (sekanta) o'tkazamiz. Ushbu to'g'ri chiziqning $Ox$ o'qi musbat yo'nalishi bilan hosil qilgan burchagi $\varphi$ bo'lsin. To'g'ri burchakli uchburchakdan uning burchak koeffitsiyenti:

$$
\tan \varphi = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{f(x_0 + \Delta x) - f(x_0)}{\Delta x}
$$

Endi $M$ nuqtani egri chiziq bo'ylab $M_0$ nuqtaga cheksiz yaqinlashtiraylik ($\Delta x \to 0$). Bu paytda $M_0 M$ kesuvchi to'g'ri chiziq o'zining chegaraviy holatiga — ya'ni egri chiziqqa $M_0$ nuqtada o'tkazilgan **urinma to'g'ri chiziqqa** aylanadi!

## 📐 Asosiy Nazariya, Qoidalar va Teoremalar

### 1. Argument va funksiya orttirmasi

- **Argument orttirmasi:** $\Delta x = x - x_0 \implies x = x_0 + \Delta x$
- **Funksiya orttirmasi:** $\Delta y = \Delta f(x_0) = f(x_0 + \Delta x) - f(x_0)$

### 2. Hosilaning qat'iy matematik ta'rifi

> **Ta'rif:** Funksiya orttirmasi $\Delta y$ ning argument orttirmasi $\Delta x$ ga nisbatining $\Delta x \to 0$ dagi chekli limiti mavjud bo'lsa, bu limit $f(x)$ funksiyaning $x_0$ nuqtadagi **hosilasi** deyiladi va $f'(x_0)$ ko'rinishida belgilanadi:
> 
> $$ f'(x_0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x_0 + \Delta x) - f(x_0)}{\Delta x}$$

**Boshqa matematik belgilashlar:**
- Leybnits belgilashi: $\frac{df}{dx}$ yoki $\left. \frac{df}{dx} \right|_{x = x_0}$
- Koshi belgilashi: $Df(x_0)$
- Lagranj belgilashi: $f'(x_0)$
- Nyuton belgilashi (fizikada vaqt bo'yicha): $\dot{x}(t)$

### 3. Hosilaning geometrik talqini

> **Teorema:** $f'(x_0)$ hosila — $y = f(x)$ egri chizig'iga $(x_0, y_0)$ nuqtada o'tkazilgan urinmaning burchak koeffitsiyenti $k$ ga teng:
> 
> $$ k = \tan \alpha = f'(x_0)$$
> 
> Bunda $\alpha$ — urinmaning $Ox$ o'qining musbat yo'nalishi bilan hosil qilgan burchagidir.

### 4. Urinma va normal to'g'ri chiziq tenglamalari

Egri chiziqqa $(x_0, y_0)$ nuqtada (bu yerda $y_0 = f(x_0)$):
1. **Urinma tenglamasi:**
   $$y - y_0 = f'(x_0)(x - x_0)$$
2. **Normal tenglamasi** (urinmaga urinish nuqtasida perpendikulyar to'g'ri chiziq):
   Agar $f'(x_0) \neq 0$ bo'lsa, perpendikulyarlik shartiga ($k_1 \cdot k_2 = -1 \implies k_{\text{norm}} = -\frac{1}{f'(x_0)}$) ko'ra:
   $$y - y_0 = -\frac{1}{f'(x_0)}(x - x_0)$$

### 5. Differensiallanuvchanlik va uzluksizlik bog'liqligi

> **Fundamental teorema:**
> Agar $f(x)$ funksiya $x_0$ nuqtada differensiallanuvchi bo'lsa (ya'ni chekli $f'(x_0)$ mavjud bo'lsa), u holda u shu nuqtada **albatta uzluksizdir**.

**Isboti:**
Hosilaning ta'rifiga ko'ra:
$$\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = f'(x_0) + \alpha(\Delta x)$$
bu yerda $\Delta x \to 0$ da $\alpha(\Delta x) \to 0$ (cheksiz kichik miqdor).
Tenglikning ikkala qismini $\Delta x$ ga ko'paytiramiz:
$$\Delta y = f'(x_0) \cdot \Delta x + \alpha(\Delta x) \cdot \Delta x$$
Endi $\Delta x \to 0$ da limitga o'tsak:
$$\lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \Delta y = f'(x_0) \cdot 0 + 0 = 0$$
Funksiya orttirmasi nolga intilishi ($\lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \Delta y = 0$) aynan funksiyaning $x_0$ nuqtada uzluksizligi ta'rifidir! $\blacksquare$

> [!CAUTION]
> **Teskari tasdiq har doim ham to'g'ri emas!**
> Funksiya nuqtada uzluksiz bo'lishi mumkin, ammo unda hosila mavjud bo'lmasligi mumkin. Masalan, $f(x) = |x|$ funksiya $x = 0$ nuqtada uzluksiz, ammo uning grafigida "sinish" (o'tkir burchak) bo'lgani sababli $x = 0$ da hosilasi mavjud emas.

### 6. Bir tomonlama hosilalar (Chap va O'ng hosila)

- **Chap hosila:** $f'_-(x_0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0^-} \frac{f(x_0 + \Delta x) - f(x_0)}{\Delta x}$
- **O'ng hosila:** $f'_+(x_0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0^+} \frac{f(x_0 + \Delta x) - f(x_0)}{\Delta x}$

Funksiya $x_0$ nuqtada differensiallanuvchi bo'lishi uchun $f'_-(x_0) = f'_+(x_0)$ tenglik bajarilishi zarur va yetarlidir.

## 📝 Qadamma-qadam Yechilgan Universitet Misollari

### 1-misol: Hosilani ta'rif bo'yicha keltirib chiqarish

**Shart:** $f(x) = \sqrt{x}$ ($x > 0$) funksiyaning hosilasini ta'rif orqali keltirib chiqaring.

**Yechish:**
1. Argumentga $\Delta x$ orttirma berib, mos funksiya orttirmasini yozamiz:
   $$\Delta y = f(x + \Delta x) - f(x) = \sqrt{x + \Delta x} - \sqrt{x}$$
2. Orttirmalar nisbatini tuzamiz:
   $$\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{\sqrt{x + \Delta x} - \sqrt{x}}{\Delta x}$$
3. Kasrning surati va maxrajini suratning qo'shmasiga, ya'ni $(\sqrt{x + \Delta x} + \sqrt{x})$ ga ko'paytiramiz:
   $$\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{(\sqrt{x + \Delta x} - \sqrt{x})(\sqrt{x + \Delta x} + \sqrt{x})}{\Delta x (\sqrt{x + \Delta x} + \sqrt{x})} = \frac{(x + \Delta x) - x}{\Delta x (\sqrt{x + \Delta x} + \sqrt{x})} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta x (\sqrt{x + \Delta x} + \sqrt{x})}$$
4. $\Delta x \neq 0$ bo'lgani uchun qisqartiramiz:
   $$\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{x + \Delta x} + \sqrt{x}}$$
5. $\Delta x \to 0$ da limitga o'tamiz:
   $$f'(x) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{1}{\sqrt{x + \Delta x} + \sqrt{x}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{x + 0} + \sqrt{x}} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}$$

**Javob:** $(\sqrt{x})' = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}$ formulasi to'liq isbotlandi.

### 2-misol: Urinma va normal tenglamalarini to'liq tuzish

**Shart:** $f(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x + 1$ egri chizig'iga $x_0 = 1$ nuqtada o'tkazilgan urinma va normal to'g'ri chiziqlar tenglamalarini tuzing.

**Yechish:**
1. Urinish nuqtasining ordinatasini hisoblaymiz:
   $$y_0 = f(1) = 1^3 - 3(1^2) + 2(1) + 1 = 1 - 3 + 2 + 1 = 1$$
   Demak, urinish nuqtasi: $M_0(1, 1)$.

2. Funksiyaning hosilasini topamiz:
   $$f'(x) = (x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x + 1)' = 3x^2 - 6x + 2$$

3. Urinmaning burchak koeffitsiyentini hisoblaymiz:
   $$k_{\text{ur}} = f'(1) = 3(1^2) - 6(1) + 2 = 3 - 6 + 2 = -1$$

4. **Urinma tenglamasini** tuzamiz:
   $$y - y_0 = k_{\text{ur}}(x - x_0) \implies y - 1 = -1 \cdot (x - 1) \implies y - 1 = -x + 1 \implies y = -x + 2$$

5. **Normal tenglamasini** tuzamiz:
   Normalning burchak koeffitsiyenti:
   $$k_{\text{norm}} = -\frac{1}{k_{\text{ur}}} = -\frac{1}{-1} = 1$$
   U holda normal tenglamasi:
   $$y - y_0 = k_{\text{norm}}(x - x_0) \implies y - 1 = 1 \cdot (x - 1) \implies y = x$$

**Javob:** Urinma tenglamasi: $y = -x + 2$; Normal tenglamasi: $y = x$.

### 3-misol: Nuqtada differensiallanuvchanlikni tekshirish

**Shart:** Ushbu funksiyaning $x = 0$ nuqtadagi hosilasini tekshiring:
$$f(x) = \begin{cases} x^2 \sin\left(\frac{1}{x}\right), & x \neq 0 \\ 0, & x = 0 \end{cases}$$

**Yechish:**
1. Standart hosila formulalarini to'g'ridan-to'g'ri qo'llash $x = 0$ da noaniqlik beradi, chunki $\frac{1}{0}$ mavjud emas. Shuning uchun qat'iy ta'rif bo'yicha limitni hisoblaymiz.
2. Ta'rifga ko'ra:
   $$f'(0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(0 + \Delta x) - f(0)}{\Delta x}$$
3. Qiymatlarni qo'yamiz: $f(0) = 0$ va $f(\Delta x) = (\Delta x)^2 \sin\left(\frac{1}{\Delta x}\right)$:
   $$\frac{f(\Delta x) - f(0)}{\Delta x} = \frac{(\Delta x)^2 \sin\left(\frac{1}{\Delta x}\right) - 0}{\Delta x} = \Delta x \cdot \sin\left(\frac{1}{\Delta x}\right)$$
4. $\Delta x \to 0$ da limitga o'tamiz:
   Biz bilamizki, $\sin\left(\frac{1}{\Delta x}\right)$ chegaralangan funksiya ($-1 \le \sin \le 1$), $\Delta x$ esa cheksiz kichik miqdor.
   Cheksiz kichik miqdorning chegaralangan miqdorga ko'paytmasi doimo cheksiz kichik miqdordir:
   $$\lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \left[ \Delta x \cdot \sin\left(\frac{1}{\Delta x}\right) \right] = 0$$
5. Limit chekli va nolga teng bo'lib chiqdi.

**Javob:** Funksiya $x = 0$ nuqtada differensiallanuvchi va $f'(0) = 0$.

### 4-misol: Fizik kinematik masala (Tezlik va Tezlanish)

**Shart:** Moddiy nuqta to'g'ri chiziq bo'ylab $s(t) = t^3 - 6t^2 + 9t + 2$ qonuniyat bilan harakatlanmoqda ($s$ — metrda, $t$ — sekundda). 
a) Qaysi vaqt lahzalarida jism to'xtagan (oniy tezlik $0$ bo'lgan)?
b) Harakat boshlangandan to'xtash paytlarigacha bo'lgan lahzalardagi tezlanishlarni toping.

**Yechish:**
1. **Oniy tezlik** masofaning vaqt bo'yicha birinchi hosilasidir:
   $$v(t) = s'(t) = (t^3 - 6t^2 + 9t + 2)' = 3t^2 - 12t + 9$$
2. Jism to'xtagan paytda tezlik nolga teng:
   $$v(t) = 0 \iff 3t^2 - 12t + 9 = 0 \iff t^2 - 4t + 3 = 0$$
   Viyet teoremasiga ko'ra:
   $$(t - 1)(t - 3) = 0 \implies t_1 = 1\text{ s}, \quad t_2 = 3\text{ s}$$
   Demak, jism $1$-sekund va $3$-sekund lahzalarida to'xtagan (yo'nalishini o'zgartirgan).

3. **Oniy tezlanish** tezlikning birinchi (yoki masofaning ikkinchi) hosilasidir:
   $$a(t) = v'(t) = s''(t) = (3t^2 - 12t + 9)' = 6t - 12$$

4. To'xtash paytlaridagi tezlanishlarni hisoblaymiz:
   - $t_1 = 1\text{ s}$ da: $a(1) = 6(1) - 12 = -6\text{ m/s}^2$ (harakat sekinlanuvchan bo'lgan)
   - $t_2 = 3\text{ s}$ da: $a(3) = 6(3) - 12 = 18 - 12 = +6\text{ m/s}^2$ (harakat tezlanuvchan)

**Javob:** a) $t_1 = 1\text{ s}$ va $t_2 = 3\text{ s}$; b) $a(1) = -6\text{ m/s}^2$, $a(3) = 6\text{ m/s}^2$.

## ⚠️ Talabalar Eng Ko'p Yo'l Qo'yadigan Xatolar va Tuzoqlar

1. **Hosilani hisoblashdan oldin nuqtani qo'yib yuborish:**
   Eng keng tarqalgan xato! Talaba $f(x) = x^3$ funksiyaning $x = 2$ dagi hosilasini hisoblashda avval $f(2) = 8$ deb oladi, so'ngra $(8)' = 0$ deb yozadi. 
   **Qat'iy qoida:** Avval umumiy $f'(x)$ hosila funksiyasi topiladi ($3x^2$), undan keyingina $x_0 = 2$ nuqta qo'yiladi ($3 \cdot 2^2 = 12$)!

2. **"Uzluksiz bo'lsa, demak hosilasi bor" deb o'ylash:**
   $f(x) = |x|$ funksiya $x = 0$ da uzluksiz, ammo chap hosilasi $f'_-(0) = -1$, o'ng hosilasi $f'_+(0) = +1$. Ular teng emas, binobarin $x = 0$ da hosila **mavjud emas**. Grafigi sinish burchagiga ega nuqtalarda hosila bo'lmaydi!

3. **Normal tenglamasida burchak koeffitsiyentini noto'g'ri olish:**
   Talabalar ko'pincha normal uchun $k_{\text{norm}} = -f'(x_0)$ deb yozib qo'yishadi. To'g'ri formula: $k_{\text{norm}} = -\frac{1}{f'(x_0)}$. Faqat teskari va qarama-qarshi ishorali songina perpendikulyarlik shartini beradi.

4. **Hosilani urinmaning to'liq tenglamasi bilan tenglashtirish:**
   $f'(x_0)$ — bu to'g'ri chiziq emas, bu faqat urinmaning qiyaligi (son). To'liq urinma tenglamasi esa $y = kx + b$ ko'rinishidagi to'g'ri chiziqdir.

## 🎯 Mustaqil Yechish Uchun Amaliy Mashqlar va Yechimlar

### 1-mashq
$f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$ ($x \neq 0$) funksiya hosilasini qat'iy limit ta'rifi orqali keltirib chiqaring.

<details>
<summary>Yechimni ko'rish</summary>

**Bosqichma-bosqich yechim:**
1. Funksiya orttirmasini tuzamiz:
   $$\Delta y = f(x + \Delta x) - f(x) = \frac{1}{x + \Delta x} - \frac{1}{x} = \frac{x - (x + \Delta x)}{x(x + \Delta x)} = \frac{-\Delta x}{x(x + \Delta x)}$$
2. Orttirmalar nisbatini olamiz:
   $$\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{-\Delta x}{\Delta x \cdot x(x + \Delta x)} = \frac{-1}{x(x + \Delta x)}$$
3. $\Delta x \to 0$ da limitga o'tamiz:
   $$f'(x) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{-1}{x(x + \Delta x)} = \frac{-1}{x(x + 0)} = -\frac{1}{x^2}$$

**Javob:** $\left( \frac{1}{x} \right)' = -\frac{1}{x^2}$
</details>

### 2-mashq
$y = \frac{x + 2}{x - 1}$ giperbolasiga $x_0 = 2$ nuqtada o'tkazilgan urinma va normal tenglamalarini toping.

<details>
<summary>Yechimni ko'rish</summary>

**Bosqichma-bosqich yechim:**
1. Urinish nuqtasi koordinatasi:
   $$y_0 = y(2) = \frac{2 + 2}{2 - 1} = \frac{4}{1} = 4 \implies (2, 4)$$
2. Bo'linma hosilasi qoidasi bo'yicha hosila olamiz:
   $$y' = \frac{(x + 2)'(x - 1) - (x + 2)(x - 1)'}{(x - 1)^2} = \frac{1(x - 1) - (x + 2) \cdot 1}{(x - 1)^2} = \frac{x - 1 - x - 2}{(x - 1)^2} = \frac{-3}{(x - 1)^2}$$
3. $x_0 = 2$ nuqtadagi hosila (urinmaning burchak koeffitsiyenti):
   $$k_{\text{ur}} = y'(2) = \frac{-3}{(2 - 1)^2} = -3$$
4. Urinma tenglamasi:
   $$y - 4 = -3(x - 2) \implies y - 4 = -3x + 6 \implies y = -3x + 10$$
5. Normalning burchak koeffitsiyenti:
   $$k_{\text{norm}} = -\frac{1}{-3} = \frac{1}{3}$$
   Normal tenglamasi:
   $$y - 4 = \frac{1}{3}(x - 2) \implies y = \frac{1}{3}x - \frac{2}{3} + 4 \implies y = \frac{1}{3}x + \frac{10}{3}$$

**Javob:** Urinma: $y = -3x + 10$; Normal: $y = \frac{1}{3}x + \frac{10}{3}$.
</details>

### 3-mashq
$f(x) = |x - 3| + 2x$ funksiyaning $x = 3$ nuqtadagi chap va o'ng hosilalarini hisoblang. Funksiya $x = 3$ nuqtada differensiallanuvchimi?

<details>
<summary>Yechimni ko'rish</summary>

**Bosqichma-bosqich yechim:**
1. Funksiyaning $x = 3$ atrofidagi ko'rinishini ochamiz:
   - Agar $x > 3$ bo'lsa: $f(x) = (x - 3) + 2x = 3x - 3$
   - Agar $x < 3$ bo'lsa: $f(x) = -(x - 3) + 2x = -x + 3 + 2x = x + 3$
   - $f(3) = |3 - 3| + 2(3) = 6$
2. O'ng hosila ($x \to 3^+$):
   $$f'_+(3) = \lim_{x \to 3^+} \frac{f(x) - f(3)}{x - 3} = \lim_{x \to 3^+} \frac{(3x - 3) - 6}{x - 3} = \lim_{x \to 3^+} \frac{3x - 9}{x - 3} = \lim_{x \to 3^+} \frac{3(x - 3)}{x - 3} = 3$$
3. Chap hosila ($x \to 3^-$):
   $$f'_-(3) = \lim_{x \to 3^-} \frac{f(x) - f(3)}{x - 3} = \lim_{x \to 3^-} \frac{(x + 3) - 6}{x - 3} = \lim_{x \to 3^-} \frac{x - 3}{x - 3} = 1$$
4. Xulosa: $f'_+(3) = 3 \neq f'_-(3) = 1$. Bir tomonlama hosilalar o'zaro teng emas.

**Javob:** Chap hosila $1$, o'ng hosila $3$. Funksiya $x = 3$ nuqtada differensiallanuvchi emas (grafikda sinish nuqtasi mavjud).
</details>

## 💡 Imtihon Layfhaklari va Tezkor Maslahatlar

1. **Gorizontal urinma layfhaki:**
   Agar masalada "urinma $Ox$ o'qiga parallel bo'lgan nuqtani toping" deyilsa, hech ikkilanmasdan hosilani nolga tenglashtiring:
   $$f'(x) = 0$$
   Chunki gorizontal to'g'ri chiziqning burchak koeffitsiyenti $k = \tan(0^\circ) = 0$.

2. **Berilgan to'g'ri chiziqqa parallel urinma topish:**
   Agar masala $y = f(x)$ grafigiga o'tkazilgan urinma $y = 5x - 3$ to'g'ri chizig'iga parallel bo'lishini talab qilsa, ularning burchak koeffitsiyentlari teng bo'lishi kerak. Darhol quyidagi tenglamani yechasiz:
   $$f'(x_0) = 5$$
   Hosil bo'lgan $x_0$ qiymat urinish nuqtasining abssissasi bo'ladi!

3. **Differensial formulasi:**
   Kichik o'zgarishlar uchun $\Delta y \approx dy = f'(x_0) \Delta x$. Bu formula orqali murakkab sonlarning taqribiy qiymatini (masalan, $\sqrt{4.02} \approx 2 + \frac{1}{4} \cdot 0.02 = 2.005$) oson topish mumkin.
