## Kirish: Tarixdagi eng mashhur qoida

Biz 3-modulda aniqmasliklarni ochishning algebraik usullarini ko'rgan edik: ko'paytuvchilarga ajratish, qo'shmaga ko'paytirish, trigonometrik ayniyatlar. Biroq ifodada bir vaqtning o'zida ham trigonometriya, ham eksponenta, ham ko'phad qatnashsa, bunday soddalashtirishlar juda qiyinlashib ketishi mumkin.

XVIII asrda fransuz matematigi Giyom Lopital (Iogann Bernulli bilan hamkorlikda) aniqmasliklarni **hosila yordamida bir zumda yechish** usulini e'lon qildi. Bu usul hozirgi kunda universitet oliy matematika kursining eng sevimli qurolidir!

---

## Lopital teoremasi

> **Teorema:** Agar $x \to a$ da $f(x)$ va $g(x)$ funksiyalarning ikkalasi ham nolga yoki ikkalasi ham cheksizlikka intilsa (ya'ni $\left[\frac{0}{0}\right]$ yoki $\left[\frac{\infty}{\infty}\right]$ noaniqlik yuz bersa) va ularning hosilalari nisbati mavjud bo'lsa, u holda funksiyalar nisbatining limiti ularning **hosilalari nisbatining limiti**ga teng bo'ladi:
>
> $$
> \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}
> $$

> [!CAUTION]
> **Eng muhim ogohlantirish:** Bu yerda bo'linmaning hosilasi $\left(\frac{u}{v}\right)'$ formulasi qo'llanilmaydi! Surat alohida differensiallanadi ($f'(x)$), maxraj esa alohida differensiallanadi ($g'(x)$)!

---

## Qoidani takroran qo'llash

Agar bir marta differensiallagandan keyin ham yana $\left[\frac{0}{0}\right]$ yoki $\left[\frac{\infty}{\infty}\right]$ aniqmaslik saqlanib qolsa, qoidani ikkinchi, uchinchi va kerak bo'lsa bir necha bor ketma-ket qo'llash mumkin:

$$
\lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f''(x)}{g''(x)}
$$

---

## Boshqa noaniqliklarni keltirish

Agar noaniqlik $[0 \cdot \infty]$ yoki $[\infty - \infty]$ ko'rinishida bo'lsa:
- $[0 \cdot \infty]$ uchun: $A \cdot B = \frac{A}{1/B}$ shaklida yozilib, $\left[\frac{0}{0}\right]$ yoki $\left[\frac{\infty}{\infty}\right]$ ga keltiriladi;
- So'ngra Lopital qoidasi qo'llaniladi.

---

## Amaliy misollar va yechimlar

### 1-misol: Qoidani ikki marta ketma-ket qo'llash
Limitni hisoblang:

$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - x - 1}{x^2}
$$

**Yechilishi:**
1. $x = 0$ qo'yamiz:
   $$\frac{e^0 - 0 - 1}{0^2} = \frac{1 - 1}{0} = \left[\frac{0}{0}\right]$$
2. Lopital qoidasini qo'llaymiz (surat va maxrajdan alohida hosila olamiz):
   $$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{(e^x - x - 1)'}{(x^2)'} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1}{2x}$$
3. Yana tekshiramiz: $x = 0$ da $\frac{e^0 - 1}{2(0)} = \left[\frac{0}{0}\right]$. Yana noaniqlik!
4. Lopital qoidasini ikkinchi marta qo'llaymiz:
   $$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{(e^x - 1)'}{(2x)'} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x}{2}$$
5. Endi bevosita $x = 0$ qo'yish mumkin:
   $$\frac{e^0}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$$

**Javob:** $\frac{1}{2}$

---

### 2-misol: $\left[\frac{\infty}{\infty}\right]$ holati
Limitni hisoblang:

$$
\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\ln x}{x^2}
$$

**Yechilishi:**
$x \to +\infty$ da $\ln x \to +\infty$ va $x^2 \to +\infty$, ya'ni $\left[\frac{\infty}{\infty}\right]$.

Lopital qoidasi bo'yicha:
$$\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{(\ln x)'}{(x^2)'} = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\frac{1}{x}}{2x} = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{1}{2x^2}$$

Maxraj cheksiz kattalashgani sari, kasr nolga intiladi:
$$\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{1}{2x^2} = 0$$

*(Xulosa: Ko'phad logarifmga qaraganda ancha tez o'sadi!)*

**Javob:** $0$

---

### 3-misol: $[0 \cdot \infty]$ aniqmasligi
Limitni hisoblang:

$$
\lim_{x \to 0^+} (x \cdot \ln x)
$$

**Yechilishi:**
$x \to 0^+$ da $x \to 0$, lekin $\ln x \to -\infty$. Bu $[0 \cdot (-\infty)]$ aniqmaslikdir.

$x$ ni maxrajga $\frac{1}{x}$ qilib tushiramiz:
$$x \cdot \ln x = \frac{\ln x}{\frac{1}{x}}$$

Endi bu $\left[\frac{-\infty}{+\infty}\right]$ ko'rinishiga keldi. Lopital qoidasini qo'llaymiz:
$$\lim_{x \to 0^+} \frac{(\ln x)'}{\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)'} = \lim_{x \to 0^+} \frac{\frac{1}{x}}{-\frac{1}{x^2}} = \lim_{x \to 0^+} \left( \frac{1}{x} \cdot (-x^2) \right) = \lim_{x \to 0^+} (-x) = 0$$

**Javob:** $0$

1. $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(3x)}{x}$ (Lopital qoidasi bilan tekshiring).
2. $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x - \sin x}{x^3}$
3. $\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{x^2}{e^x}$
