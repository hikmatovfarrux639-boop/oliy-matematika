## Kirish: Trigonometriya oliy matematikada nima uchun kerak?

Oliy matematikada burchaklar deyarli har doim **radianlarda** o'lchanadi. Trigonometrik funksiyalar:
- 1-ajoyib limitda: $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1$;
- Trigonometrik integrallarda: $\int \sin^m x \cos^n x \, dx$;
- Kompleks sonlarning trigonometrik va Eyler shaklida: $z = r(\cos \varphi + i\sin \varphi)$;
- Furye qatorlarida va to'lqin jarayonlarini ifodalashda
asosiy poydevor hisoblanadi.

---

## 1. Radian o'lchovi va Birlik aylana

Trigonometrik funksiyalarning umumiy ta'rifi markazi koordinatalar boshida bo'lgan birlik aylana ($R = 1$) orqali beriladi:
- Gorizontal o'q ($Ox$) — **kosinuslar o'qi** ($\cos \alpha$);
- Vertikal o'q ($Oy$) — **sinuslar o'qi** ($\sin \alpha$).

### Radian va gradus bog'liqligi:
$$
\pi \text{ rad} = 180^\circ \implies 1 \text{ rad} = \frac{180^\circ}{\pi} \approx 57.3^\circ
$$

| Gradus | $0^\circ$ | $30^\circ$ | $45^\circ$ | $60^\circ$ | $90^\circ$ | $180^\circ$ | $270^\circ$ | $360^\circ$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Radian | $0$ | $\frac{\pi}{6}$ | $\frac{\pi}{4}$ | $\frac{\pi}{3}$ | $\frac{\pi}{2}$ | $\pi$ | $\frac{3\pi}{2}$ | $2\pi$ |

## 2. Asosiy trigonometrik ayniyatlar

1. **Pifagor ayniyati:**
   $$
   \sin^2 \alpha + \cos^2 \alpha = 1
   $$
2. Tangens va kotangens ta'rifi:
   $$
   \operatorname{tg} \alpha = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha}, \qquad \operatorname{ctg} \alpha = \frac{\cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha}
   $$
3. O'zaro ko'paytmasi:
   $$
   \operatorname{tg} \alpha \cdot \operatorname{ctg} \alpha = 1 \quad \left(\alpha \neq \frac{\pi k}{2}\right)
   $$
4. Tangens va kosinus bog'liqligi:
   $$
   1 + \operatorname{tg}^2 \alpha = \frac{1}{\cos^2 \alpha}, \qquad 1 + \operatorname{ctg}^2 \alpha = \frac{1}{\sin^2 \alpha}
   $$

## 3. Qo'shish va karrali burchak formulalari

Integrallash va limitlarni hisoblashda eng ko'p zarur bo'ladigan ayniyatlar:

### Qo'shish formulalari:
$$
\sin(\alpha \pm \beta) = \sin \alpha \cos \beta \pm \cos \alpha \sin \beta
$$
$$
\cos(\alpha \pm \beta) = \cos \alpha \cos \beta \mp \sin \alpha \sin \beta
$$

### Ikkilangan burchak formulalari:
$$
\sin(2\alpha) = 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha
$$
$$
\cos(2\alpha) = \cos^2 \alpha - \sin^2 \alpha = 2\cos^2 \alpha - 1 = 1 - 2\sin^2 \alpha
$$

### Darajani pasaytirish formulalari (Integrallar uchun juda muhim!):
$$
\sin^2 \alpha = \frac{1 - \cos(2\alpha)}{2}
$$
$$
\cos^2 \alpha = \frac{1 + \cos(2\alpha)}{2}
$$

> **Maslahat:** $\int \sin^2 x \, dx$ yoki $\int \cos^2 x \, dx$ integrallarini hisoblashda darajani pasaytirish formulasisiz yechish deyarli imkonsizdir!

## 4. Ko'paytmani yig'indiga aylantirish

$$
\sin \alpha \cos \beta = \frac{1}{2}[\sin(\alpha + \beta) + \sin(\alpha - \beta)]
$$
$$
\cos \alpha \cos \beta = \frac{1}{2}[\cos(\alpha + \beta) + \cos(\alpha - \beta)]
$$
$$
\sin \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{1}{2}[\cos(\alpha - \beta) - \cos(\alpha + \beta)]
$$

---

## 5. Keltirish formulalari (Mnemotexnik qoida)

$\frac{\pi}{2} \cdot k \pm \alpha$ ko'rinishidagi burchaklar uchun ikkita oddiy savol beriladi:
1. **Funksiya nomi o'zgaradimi?**
   - Agar burchak vertikal o'qda bo'lsa ($\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{3\pi}{2}$ — ya'ni $k$ toq bo'lsa), bosh chayqaladi: **HA, o'zgaradi** ($\sin \leftrightarrow \cos$, $\operatorname{tg} \leftrightarrow \operatorname{ctg}$).
   - Agar burchak gorizontal o'qda bo'lsa ($\pi, 2\pi$ — ya'ni $k$ juft bo'lsa), bosh gorizontal chayqaladi: **YO'Q, o'zgarmaydi**.
2. **Oldidagi ishora qanday bo'ladi?**
   - Dastlabki burchak joylashgan chorakdagi **dastlabki funksiyaning** ishorasi qo'yiladi.

**Misol:** $\cos\left(\frac{3\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)$.
- $\frac{3\pi}{2}$ vertikal o'qda $\implies \cos \to \sin$.
- $\frac{3\pi}{2} - \alpha$ — 3-chorak. 3-chorakda kosinus manfiy ($-$).
- Natija: $-\sin \alpha$.

---

## 6. Asosiy trigonometrik tenglamalar

1. $\sin x = a \quad (\lvert a \rvert \le 1)$:
   $$
   x = (-1)^k \arcsin a + \pi k, \quad k \in \mathbb{Z}
   $$
2. $\cos x = a \quad (\lvert a \rvert \le 1)$:
   $$
   x = \pm \arccos a + 2\pi k, \quad k \in \mathbb{Z}
   $$
3. $\operatorname{tg} x = a \quad (a \in \mathbb{R})$:
   $$
   x = \operatorname{arctg} a + \pi k, \quad k \in \mathbb{Z}
   $$

## Mustaqil yechish uchun mashqlar

2. $\cos(2x) = 0$ tenglamaning barcha yechimlarini toping.
3. Keltirish formulasi yordamida soddalashtiring: $\sin(\pi + \alpha) + \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)$.
