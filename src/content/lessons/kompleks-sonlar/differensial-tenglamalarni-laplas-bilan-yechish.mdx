## Kirish: O'zgaruvchilarni yo'qotish va operatsion mo''jiza

Klassik matematik analizda ikkinchi tartibli differensial tenglamani yoki differensial tenglamalar sistemasini boshlang'ich shartlar bilan (Koshi masalasi) yechish juda ko'p qadamlarni talab qiladi:
1. Bir jinsli tenglama umumiy yechimi topiladi ($C_1, C_2$ bilan);
2. Bir jinsli bo'lmagan qism uchun xususiy yechim tanlanadi;
3. Umumiy yechim yozilib, hosilasi olinadi;
4. Boshlang'ich shartlar qo'yilib, $C_1, C_2$ ga nisbatan chiziqli sistema yechiladi.

**Laplas almashtirishi** esa bu 4 ta bosqichni bitta jarayonga birlashtiradi: boshlang'ich shartlar eng boshidayoq formulaga kiritiladi va differensial tenglama oddiy **chiziqli algebraik tenglamaga** aylanadi!

---

## 1. Hosilaning tasviri formulalari

Agar $y(t) \fallingdotseq Y(p)$ bo'lsa, uning hosilalarining tasvirlari quyidagicha bo'ladi:
$$
y'(t) \fallingdotseq p Y(p) - y(0)
$$
$$
y''(t) \fallingdotseq p^2 Y(p) - p \cdot y(0) - y'(0)
$$
$$
y'''(t) \fallingdotseq p^3 Y(p) - p^2 y(0) - p y'(0) - y''(0)
$$

### Boshlang'ich funksiyalar tasvirlari jadvali:
| Original $f(t)$ | Tasvir $F(p)$ |
| :--- | :--- |
| $1$ | $\frac{1}{p}$ |
| $t^n$ | $\frac{n!}{p^{n+1}}$ |
| $e^{at}$ | $\frac{1}{p - a}$ |
| $\sin(\omega t)$ | $\frac{\omega}{p^2 + \omega^2}$ |
| $\cos(\omega t)$ | $\frac{p}{p^2 + \omega^2}$ |
| $e^{at}\sin(\omega t)$ | $\frac{\omega}{(p-a)^2 + \omega^2}$ |
| $e^{at}\cos(\omega t)$ | $\frac{p-a}{(p-a)^2 + \omega^2}$ |

## 2. Differensial tenglamani yechish sxemasi

```
 Differensial tenglama:                 Operator tenglama:
 a y'' + b y' + c y = f(t)   ----->   (a p^2 + b p + c) Y(p) - P_0(p) = F(p)
    (Koshi shartlari bilan)  Laplas            |
                                               | Algebraik yo'l bilan
                                               V
 Original yechim:                       Tasvir yechim:
     y(t)                  <-----           Y(p) = A(p) / B(p)
                             Teskari
                             Laplas
```

## 3. Differensial tenglamalar sistemalarini yechish

Agar sistema berilgan bo'lsa:
$$
\begin{cases}
x' = a_{11} x + a_{12} y + f_1(t), \quad x(0) = x_0 \\
y' = a_{21} x + a_{22} y + f_2(t), \quad y(0) = y_0
\end{cases}
$$
Laplas almashtirishidan so'ng $X(p)$ va $Y(p)$ larga nisbatan 2 ta chiziqli algebraik tenglama hosil bo'ladi:
$$
\begin{cases}
(p - a_{11}) X(p) - a_{12} Y(p) = x_0 + F_1(p) \\
-a_{21} X(p) + (p - a_{22}) Y(p) = y_0 + F_2(p)
\end{cases}
$$
Ushbu sistemani Kramer qoidasi yoki determinantlar yordamida yechib, $X(p)$ va $Y(p)$ topiladi, so'ngra sodda kasrlarga ajratish orqali $x(t), y(t)$ tiklanadi.

## 4. Masalalarni 2 xil usulda tahlil qilish

### Masala:
Koshi masalasini yeching:
$$
y'' - 3y' + 2y = 4e^{3t}, \quad y(0) = 1, \quad y'(0) = 2
$$

#### 1-usul: Laplas almashtirishi usuli (Operatsion hisob)
Hosilalar tasvirini qo'yamiz:
- $y \fallingdotseq Y(p)$
- $y' \fallingdotseq p Y(p) - y(0) = p Y(p) - 1$
- $y'' \fallingdotseq p^2 Y(p) - p y(0) - y'(0) = p^2 Y(p) - p - 2$
- $4e^{3t} \fallingdotseq \frac{4}{p-3}$

Tenglamani tasvirda yozamiz:
$$
(p^2 Y(p) - p - 2) - 3(p Y(p) - 1) + 2Y(p) = \frac{4}{p-3}
$$
$Y(p)$ ni qavsdan tashqariga chiqaramiz:
$$
(p^2 - 3p + 2) Y(p) - p - 2 + 3 = \frac{4}{p-3}
$$
$$
(p^2 - 3p + 2) Y(p) - p + 1 = \frac{4}{p-3}
$$
$$
(p-1)(p-2) Y(p) = p - 1 + \frac{4}{p-3} = \frac{(p-1)(p-3) + 4}{p-3} = \frac{p^2 - 4p + 7}{p-3}
$$
Demak:
$$
Y(p) = \frac{p^2 - 4p + 7}{(p-1)(p-2)(p-3)}
$$
Ushbu ratsional kasrni Heaviside usuli (yopish usuli) bilan sodda kasrlarga ajratamiz:
$$
\frac{p^2 - 4p + 7}{(p-1)(p-2)(p-3)} = \frac{A}{p-1} + \frac{B}{p-2} + \frac{C}{p-3}
$$
- $A$ ni topish uchun $(p-1)$ ni yopib, $p=1$ qo'yamiz:
  $$
  A = \frac{1^2 - 4(1) + 7}{(1-2)(1-3)} = \frac{4}{(-1)(-2)} = \frac{4}{2} = 2
  $$
- $B$ ni topish uchun $(p-2)$ ni yopib, $p=2$ qo'yamiz:
  $$
  B = \frac{2^2 - 4(2) + 7}{(2-1)(2-3)} = \frac{4 - 8 + 7}{(1)(-1)} = \frac{3}{-1} = -3
  $$
- $C$ ni topish uchun $(p-3)$ ni yopib, $p=3$ qo'yamiz:
  $$
  C = \frac{3^2 - 4(3) + 7}{(3-1)(3-2)} = \frac{9 - 12 + 7}{(2)(1)} = \frac{4}{2} = 2
  $$
Demak:
$$
Y(p) = \frac{2}{p-1} - \frac{3}{p-2} + \frac{2}{p-3}
$$
Jadval orqali originalga qaytamiz:
$$
y(t) = 2e^t - 3e^{2t} + 2e^{3t}
$$

---

#### 2-usul: Klassik differensial tenglamalar usuli
1. Xarakteristik tenglama: $k^2 - 3k + 2 = 0 \implies k_1 = 1, k_2 = 2$.
   Bir jinsli yechim: $y_0 = C_1 e^t + C_2 e^{2t}$.
2. Xususiy yechim: o'ng tomon $4e^{3t}$ bo'lib, $\gamma = 3$ ildiz emas.
   $y_x = A e^{3t}$.
   Hosilalar: $y_x' = 3A e^{3t}, y_x'' = 9A e^{3t}$.
   Tenglamaga qo'yamiz: $(9A - 9A + 2A)e^{3t} = 4e^{3t} \implies 2A = 4 \implies A = 2$.
   Umumiy yechim: $y(t) = C_1 e^t + C_2 e^{2t} + 2e^{3t}$.
3. Hosilasi: $y'(t) = C_1 e^t + 2C_2 e^{2t} + 6e^{3t}$.
4. Boshlang'ich shartlarni qo'yamiz:
   $$
   \begin{cases} y(0) = C_1 + C_2 + 2 = 1 \implies C_1 + C_2 = -1 \\ y'(0) = C_1 + 2C_2 + 6 = 2 \implies C_1 + 2C_2 = -4 \end{cases}
   $$
   Ayirsak: $C_2 = -3 \implies C_1 = -1 - (-3) = 2$.
   Natijada:
   $$
   y(t) = 2e^t - 3e^{2t} + 2e^{3t}
   $$
Ikkala usul ham aynan bir xil natija berdi! Ko'rib turganingizdek, Laplas usulida hech qanday sistema tuzmasdan, $C_1, C_2$ larni alohida topmasdan yechim biryo'la chiqadi.

---

> [!WARNING] Imtihondagi tuzoqlar va ko'p uchraydigan xatoliklar
> 1. **Boshlang'ich shartlar ishoralari:** Hosila formulasida minus ishora bor: $y'' \fallingdotseq p^2 Y(p) - p y(0) - y'(0)$. Agar $y(0)$ yoki $y'(0)$ manfiy son bo'lsa, ikkita minus plyus bo'lishini e'tibordan chetda qoldirmang.
> 2. **Kasrlarga ajratishda Heaviside qoidasi:** Heaviside yopish usuli faqat maxrajdagi qavslar 1-darajali va takrorlanmaydigan bo'lganda to'g'ridan-to'g'ri ishlaydi. Agar $(p-1)^2$ kabi karrali qavslar bo'lsa, noma'lum koeffitsiyentlar usulidan foydalanish zarur.

## Mustaqil yechish uchun mashqlar

### 1-mashq
Koshi masalasini Laplas almashtirishi yordamida yeching:
$$
y' + 2y = 4, \quad y(0) = 1
$$

<details>
<summary>👉 Yechimni tekshirish</summary>

**Yechish:**
$p Y(p) - 1 + 2 Y(p) = \frac{4}{p}$.
$(p+2) Y(p) = 1 + \frac{4}{p} = \frac{p+4}{p}$.
$$
Y(p) = \frac{p+4}{p(p+2)} = \frac{A}{p} + \frac{B}{p+2}
$$
$A = \frac{0+4}{0+2} = 2$.
$B = \frac{-2+4}{-2} = -1$.
$Y(p) = \frac{2}{p} - \frac{1}{p+2} \implies y(t) = 2 - e^{-2t}$.

**Javob:** $y(t) = 2 - e^{-2t}$.
</details>

### 2-mashq
Koshi masalasini Laplas almashtirishi yordamida yeching:
$$
y'' + y = 0, \quad y(0) = 0, \quad y'(0) = 3
$$

<details>
<summary>👉 Yechimni tekshirish</summary>

**Yechish:**
$p^2 Y(p) - p(0) - 3 + Y(p) = 0$.
$(p^2 + 1) Y(p) = 3 \implies Y(p) = \frac{3}{p^2 + 1}$.
Jadvaldan $\frac{1}{p^2+1} \fallingdotseq \sin t$ ekanligidan:
$$
y(t) = 3\sin t
$$

**Javob:** $y(t) = 3\sin t$.
</details>
