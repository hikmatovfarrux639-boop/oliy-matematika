## Kirish: Katta darajalar va ildizlar siri

Agar bizdan $(1 + i)^{20}$ sonini hisoblash talab qilinsa, Nyuton binomi orqali yoyib chiqish juda ko'p vaqt va chalkashlikni talab qiladi.

Fransuz matematigi Avraam de Muavr (Abraham de Moivre) trigonometrik shakldan foydalanib, har qanday kompleks sonni istalgan $n$-darajaga ko'tarish va undan $n$-darajali ildiz chiqarishning universal formulasini kashf qildi.

---

## Muavr formulasi ($z^n$)

> **Muavr formulasi:** Agar $z = r(\cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi)$ kompleks son berilgan bo'lsa, uning $n$-darajasi ($n \in \mathbb{Z}$) quyidagicha hisoblanadi:
>
> $$
> z^n = r^n [\cos(n\varphi) + i\sin(n\varphi)]
> $$
>
> yoki ko'rsatkichli shaklda:
>
> $$
> z^n = (r e^{i\varphi})^n = r^n e^{i n\varphi}
> $$

**Qoida:** Kompleks sonni $n$-darajaga ko'tarish uchun uning **moduli $n$-darajaga ko'tariladi**, **argumenti esa $n$ ga ko'paytiriladi**!

---

## Kompleks sondan $n$-darajali ildiz chiqarish

Haqiqiy sonlar sohasida musbat sondan faqat bitta haqiqiy ildiz chiqadi (masalan, $\sqrt{4} = 2$). 
Ammo kompleks sonlar maydonida **Algebraning asosiy teoremasi**ga ko'ra:

> **Ildiz chiqarish formulasi:** Noldan farqli ixtiyoriy $z = r(\cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi)$ kompleks sonidan olingan $n$-darajali ildiz **aynan $n$ ta turli kompleks qiymatga** ega bo'ladi:
>
> $$
> w_k = \sqrt[n]{z} = \sqrt[n]{r} \left( \cos\frac{\varphi + 2\pi k}{n} + i\sin\frac{\varphi + 2\pi k}{n} \right)
> $$
>
> bu yerda $k = 0, 1, 2, \dots, n-1$.

---

## Ildizlarning ajoyib geometrik shakli

Bu formuladan hayratlanarli geometrik xulosa kelib chiqadi:
- Barcha $n$ ta ildizning moduli bir xil: $|w_k| = \sqrt[n]{r}$. Demak, ularning barchasi markazi koordinata boshida va radiusi $R = \sqrt[n]{r}$ bo'lgan **bitta aylanada yotadi**!
- Ketma-ket kelgan ildizlar orasidagi burchak $\frac{2\pi}{n}$ ga teng.
- Bu degani: barcha $n$ ta ildiz kompleks tekislikda **muntazam $n$-burchakning uchlari**ni hosil qiladi!
  - 3 ta ildiz bo'lsa: *teng tomonli uchburchak*;
  - 4 ta ildiz bo'lsa: *aylanaga ichki chizilgan kvadrat*;
  - 6 ta ildiz bo'lsa: *muntazam oltiburchak*!

## Amaliy misollar va yechimlar

### 1-misol: Muavr formulasi orqali darajaga ko'tarish
$(1 + i)^{10}$ ifodaning qiymatini hisoblang.

**Yechilishi:**
1. Avval $z = 1 + i$ sonini trigonometrik shaklga o'tkazamiz:
   - Modul: $r = \sqrt{1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2}$
   - Argument: I chorak, $\varphi = \frac{\pi}{4}$
   - $z = \sqrt{2} \left(\cos\frac{\pi}{4} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$
2. Muavr formulasini qo'llaymiz ($n = 10$):
   $$z^{10} = (\sqrt{2})^{10} \left[ \cos\left(10 \cdot \frac{\pi}{4}\right) + i\sin\left(10 \cdot \frac{\pi}{4}\right) \right]$$
   - $(\sqrt{2})^{10} = 2^5 = 32$
   - $10 \cdot \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{5\pi}{2} = 2\pi + \frac{\pi}{2}$
   - $\cos\frac{5\pi}{2} = \cos\frac{\pi}{2} = 0$
   - $\sin\frac{5\pi}{2} = \sin\frac{\pi}{2} = 1$
3. Natija:
   $$z^{10} = 32(0 + 1i) = 32i$$

**Javob:** $32i$

---

### 2-misol: Birdan kubik ildiz chiqarish ($\sqrt[3]{1}$)
$\sqrt[3]{1}$ ning barcha kompleks qiymatlarini toping.

**Yechilishi:**
$z = 1$ sonining moduli $r = 1$, argumenti $\varphi = 0$.
Formula bo'yicha ($n = 3, k \in \{0, 1, 2\}$):
$$w_k = \sqrt[3]{1} \left( \cos\frac{0 + 2\pi k}{3} + i\sin\frac{0 + 2\pi k}{3} \right) = \cos\frac{2\pi k}{3} + i\sin\frac{2\pi k}{3}$$

1. $k = 0$ bo'lganda:
   $$w_0 = \cos 0 + i\sin 0 = 1$$ (oddiy haqiqiy ildiz)
2. $k = 1$ bo'lganda:
   $$w_1 = \cos\frac{2\pi}{3} + i\sin\frac{2\pi}{3} = -\frac{1}{2} + i\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$
3. $k = 2$ bo'lganda:
   $$w_2 = \cos\frac{4\pi}{3} + i\sin\frac{4\pi}{3} = -\frac{1}{2} - i\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

Bu uchta nuqta kompleks tekislikda birlik aylanaga ichki chizilgan teng tomonli uchburchakni hosil qiladi!

**Javob:** $w_0 = 1, \quad w_{1, 2} = -\frac{1}{2} \pm i\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

---

### 3-misol: To'rtinchi darajali ildiz chiqarish
$x^4 + 16 = 0$ tenglamaning barcha ildizlarini toping.

**Yechilishi:**
$x^4 = -16 \implies x = \sqrt[4]{-16}$.
$z = -16$ uchun: modul $r = 16$, argument $\varphi = \pi$.
$$w_k = \sqrt[4]{16} \left( \cos\frac{\pi + 2\pi k}{4} + i\sin\frac{\pi + 2\pi k}{4} \right) = 2 \left( \cos\frac{\pi + 2\pi k}{4} + i\sin\frac{\pi + 2\pi k}{4} \right)$$
$k = 0, 1, 2, 3$:
- $k = 0: \quad w_0 = 2\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{4} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = 2\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + i\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) = \sqrt{2} + i\sqrt{2}$
- $k = 1: \quad w_1 = 2\left(\cos\frac{3\pi}{4} + i\sin\frac{3\pi}{4}\right) = -\sqrt{2} + i\sqrt{2}$
- $k = 2: \quad w_2 = 2\left(\cos\frac{5\pi}{4} + i\sin\frac{5\pi}{4}\right) = -\sqrt{2} - i\sqrt{2}$
- $k = 3: \quad w_3 = 2\left(\cos\frac{7\pi}{4} + i\sin\frac{7\pi}{4}\right) = \sqrt{2} - i\sqrt{2}$

Bu 4 ta ildiz radiusi 2 bo'lgan kvadratning uchlaridir!

**Javob:** $\pm\sqrt{2} \pm i\sqrt{2}$

## Mustaqil yechish uchun mashqlar

1. Muavr formulasi yordamida $(1 - i)^8$ ni hisoblang.
2. $\sqrt{i}$ ning 2 ta qiymatini toping.
3. $\sqrt[3]{-8}$ ning barcha ildizlarini aniqlang.
