## Kirish: Nega algebraik shakl yetarli emas?

Agar sizdan $(1 + i\sqrt{3})^{100}$ ni hisoblash so'ralsa, qavslarni yuz marta ochib ko'paytirish deyarli imkonsiz! 
Xuddi shuningdek, kompleks sondan ildiz chiqarish algebraik shaklda juda mashaqqatli.

Shu sababli shveytsariyalik buyuk matematik Leonard Eyler kompleks sonlarni **trigonometrik** va **ko'rsatkichli** shaklga o'tkazish usulini yaratdi. Bu usul yordamida yuzinchi darajaga ko'tarish ham bir daqiqada hal bo'ladi!

---

## Modul va Argument ($\varphi$)

Kompleks tekislikda har bir $z = a + bi$ soni $(a; b)$ nuqta orqali beriladi.
Ushbu nuqtani to'g'ri burchakli koordinatalar o'rniga **qutb koordinatalari** ($r, \varphi$) orqali ifodalash mumkin:

1. **Modul ($r$):** Boshlang'ich nuqtadan nuqtagacha bo'lgan masofa:
   $$r = |z| = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$$
2. **Argument ($\varphi$):** Musbat haqiqiy yarim o'q bilan nuqtaga yo'nalgan vektor orasidagi burchak (radianda):
   $$\cos\varphi = \frac{a}{r}, \quad \sin\varphi = \frac{b}{r}$$

Burchak $\varphi = \arg(z)$ bosh argument deyiladi va odatda $(-\pi; \pi]$ oralig'ida olinadi:
- **I chorak ($a > 0, b > 0$):** $\varphi = \operatorname{arctg}\left(\frac{b}{a}\right)$
- **II chorak ($a < 0, b > 0$):** $\varphi = \pi - \operatorname{arctg}\left|\frac{b}{a}\right|$
- **III chorak ($a < 0, b < 0$):** $\varphi = -\pi + \operatorname{arctg}\left|\frac{b}{a}\right|$
- **IV chorak ($a > 0, b < 0$):** $\varphi = -\operatorname{arctg}\left|\frac{b}{a}\right|$

---

## 1. Trigonometrik shakl

$a = r\cos\varphi$ va $b = r\sin\varphi$ ekanligidan:

$$
z = r(\cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi)
$$

Bu ifoda kompleks sonning **trigonometrik shakli** deyiladi.

---

## 2. Eyler formulasi va Ko'rsatkichli shakl

Matematika tarixidagi eng buyuk kashfiyotlardan biri — bu **Eyler formulasi**dir:

$$
e^{i\varphi} = \cos\varphi + i\sin\varphi
$$

Bu formula orqali kompleks sonni nihoyatda ixcham va go'zal **ko'rsatkichli shakl**da yozish mumkin:

$$
z = r e^{i\varphi}
$$

> **Matematikaning eng go'zal tenglamasi (Eyler ayniyati):**
> Agar $\varphi = \pi$ deb olsak, $\cos\pi = -1$ va $\sin\pi = 0$ bo'lgani sababli:
> $$e^{i\pi} = -1 \iff e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$$
> Bu formulada butun matematikaning 5 ta eng muhim soni birlashgan: $e, i, \pi, 1, 0$!

---

## Geometrik ko'paytirish va bo'lish

Ko'rsatkichli shaklda ko'paytirish va bo'lish hayratlanarli darajada osonlashadi:

### Ko'paytirish:
$$z_1 \cdot z_2 = (r_1 e^{i\varphi_1}) \cdot (r_2 e^{i\varphi_2}) = (r_1 r_2) e^{i(\varphi_1 + \varphi_2)}$$

> **Qoida:** Kompleks sonlarni ko'paytirganda ularning **modullari ko'paytiriladi**, **argumentlari esa qo'shiladi**! 
> (Geometrik ma'nosi: vektor moduli cho'ziladi va $\varphi_2$ burchakka buriladi).

### Bo'lish:
$$\frac{z_1}{z_2} = \left(\frac{r_1}{r_2}\right) e^{i(\varphi_1 - \varphi_2)}$$

> **Qoida:** Bo'lganda **modullar bo'linadi**, **argumentlar ayriladi**!

## Amaliy misollar va yechimlar

### 1-misol: Trigonometrik va ko'rsatkichli shaklga o'tkazish
$z = 1 + i\sqrt{3}$ sonini trigonometrik va ko'rsatkichli shaklga o'tkazing.

**Yechilishi:**
1. $a = 1, \quad b = \sqrt{3}$.
2. Modulni topamiz:
   $$r = \sqrt{1^2 + (\sqrt{3})^2} = \sqrt{1 + 3} = \sqrt{4} = 2$$
3. Argumentni topamiz: Nuqta I chorakda joylashgan ($a > 0, b > 0$):
   $$\operatorname{tg}\varphi = \frac{b}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{1} = \sqrt{3} \implies \varphi = \frac{\pi}{3} \quad (60^\circ)$$
4. Trigonometrik shakli:
   $$z = 2\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{3} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\right)$$
5. Ko'rsatkichli shakli:
   $$z = 2 e^{i\frac{\pi}{3}}$$

---

### 2-misol: II chorakdagi sonni o'tkazish
$z = -1 + i$ sonini ko'rsatkichli shaklda yozing.

**Yechilishi:**
1. $a = -1, \quad b = 1$.
2. Modul: $r = \sqrt{(-1)^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2}$.
3. Nuqta II chorakda ($a < 0, b > 0$):
   $$\varphi = \pi - \operatorname{arctg}\left|\frac{1}{-1}\right| = \pi - \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{3\pi}{4}$$
4. Natija:
   $$z = \sqrt{2} e^{i\frac{3\pi}{4}}$$

---

### 3-misol: Tezkor ko'paytirish
$z_1 = 3 e^{i\frac{\pi}{6}}$ va $z_2 = 2 e^{i\frac{\pi}{3}}$ sonlarining ko'paytmasini toping.

**Yechilishi:**
Modullarni ko'paytiramiz: $r = 3 \cdot 2 = 6$.
Argumentlarni qo'shamiz:
$$\varphi = \frac{\pi}{6} + \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\pi + 2\pi}{6} = \frac{3\pi}{6} = \frac{\pi}{2}$$

Demak:
$$z_1 \cdot z_2 = 6 e^{i\frac{\pi}{2}} = 6\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{2} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 6(0 + i \cdot 1) = 6i$$

**Javob:** $6i$

---
